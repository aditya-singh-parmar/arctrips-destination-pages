Whale watching in Tofino

Grey whales from March, humpbacks all summer, and how to get on the water.

Somewhere around the middle of March, roughly twenty thousand grey whales pass the mouth of Clayoquot Sound on their way north. Most people miss them by two weeks.

The migration is the loud part of the season, but it is not the whole of it. A group of resident greys peels off and stays through summer, feeding in the shallows off Cow Bay. Humpbacks arrive in June and stay until the light goes. Orcas turn up unannounced.

When to come

March to May is the migration. Boats run daily, sightings are close to guaranteed, and the weather is doing whatever it likes. June to September brings calmer water, resident greys and humpbacks. October onward the tours thin out and storm season takes over.

  • March to April, greys heading north, often several at once
  • May, the tail of the migration plus the first residents settling in
  • June to September, humpbacks offshore and residents close in
  • Year round, sea lions, harbour seals and bald eagles on the way out

Where they actually are

The boats head for the same handful of places. Cow Bay on the west side of Flores Island is the reliable one for residents. In migration season the skippers push further out toward the open coast, which is why the ride gets rougher and the trip gets longer.

You do not need a boat to see one. From shore, the best odds are the headland at Cox Bay, the rocks at the end of Chesterman, and the lookout on the Wild Pacific Trail across the water in Ucluelet.

Doing it properly

Dress for one season colder than the forecast. The open water off Tofino is reliably ten degrees meaner than the harbour, and a zodiac at speed removes any warmth you were holding on to.

  • Book the first sailing of the day, the water is flattest before eleven
  • Take the covered boat in March, take the zodiac in July
  • Keep your distance from wildlife, never crowd a surfacing animal

Common questions

What is the best month?
Mid March to early May for the migration, when sightings are close to guaranteed. July and August for calm water and humpbacks.
Can you see whales from shore?
Yes, with patience. The Cox Bay headland and the end of Chesterman Beach are the two best vantage points from land.
Are the tours suitable for children?
Covered vessels take children from about five. Zodiacs are usually eight and up, and they are a rough ride in migration season.
