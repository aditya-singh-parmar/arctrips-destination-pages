Whale watching in Tofino

Somewhere around the middle of March, roughly twenty thousand grey whales pass the mouth of Clayoquot Sound on their way north. Most people miss them by two weeks.

The migration is the loud part of the season, but it is not the whole of it. A group of resident greys peels off and stays through summer, feeding in the shallows off Cow Bay.

When to come

March to May is the migration. Boats run daily and sightings are close to guaranteed. June to September brings calmer water, resident greys and humpbacks.

  • March to April, greys heading north, often several at once
  • June to September, humpbacks offshore and residents close in

Where they actually are

Cow Bay on the west side of Flores Island is the reliable one for residents. In migration season the skippers push further out toward the open coast.
