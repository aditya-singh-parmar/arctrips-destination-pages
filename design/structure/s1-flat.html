Whale watching in Tofino

Somewhere around the middle of March, roughly twenty thousand grey whales pass the mouth of Clayoquot Sound on their way north. Most people miss them by two weeks.

The migration is the loud part of the season, but it is not the whole of it. A group of resident greys peels off and stays through summer, feeding in the shallows off Cow Bay. Humpbacks arrive in June and stay until the light goes.

When to come

March to May is the migration. Boats run daily, sightings are close to guaranteed, and the weather is doing whatever it likes. June to September brings calmer water, resident greys and humpbacks.

  • March to April, greys heading north, often several at once
  • June to September, humpbacks offshore and residents close in
  • Year round, sea lions, harbour seals and bald eagles

Where they actually are

The boats head for the same handful of places. Cow Bay on the west side of Flores Island is the reliable one for residents. In migration season the skippers push further out toward the open coast.

Common questions

What is the best month?
Mid March to early May for the migration. July and August for calm water and humpbacks.
Can you see whales from shore?
Yes, with patience. Cox Bay headland and the end of Chesterman are the best vantage points.
