Three directions for /destinations

Same locked palette, same Inter, same voice. What differs is what the page leads with, and therefore what it argues this product is.

Leads with the calendar

Opens on a ruled ledger of every subject we cover, ordered by how well this month suits it, with the twelve-cell season strip on every row. Destinations come second.

What is fixed across all three. Azure is action only, Emerald is the season and availability signal only, navy is ink. Inter throughout, Satoshi for the wordmark. No italics, no em dashes, no emoji, one primary button per screen.

What is real. Every figure comes from the current database: 13 guides, 127 documented places, 292 stays, Tofino from $320 a night, fishing bookable and whale watching not yet. Month data is the real best_months. The concepts are set in July, so the outlined cell and the "Now" markers move with the calendar.

What I would pick, and why. C for the page that ships, because it is the only one that still works at 24 destinations without a rethink, and because the corpus is its real asset. But A owns the strongest single idea in the product, the almanac, and that ledger deserves to exist somewhere even if the index is C. B's comparison table is the answer to the most common question on the site and belongs on a Tofino-versus-Ucluelet page, which the taxonomy already reserves at /destinations/compare/.

These are static mockups. Nothing here is wired to the app yet.
