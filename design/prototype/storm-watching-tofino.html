Storm watching in Tofino

Tofino is not just a summer beach town. In winter, it becomes one of the best places in Canada to watch storms.

Cox Bay

Best for: Big waves, beach views, photos, stormy ocean scenes

Good to know. Stay far back from the water. Waves can reach higher up the beach than expected.

Chesterman Beach

Best for: Easy access, beach walks, families, photography

Good to know. Watch the tide. Parts of the beach may be harder to walk during high tide.

Long Beach

Long Beach is part of Pacific Rim National Park Reserve. It is one of the most famous beaches on Vancouver Island. It is wide, long, and open to the ocean. This makes it a powerful place to watch wint

Best for: Wide views, long walks, classic west coast scenery

Good to know. You may need a park pass. Always follow Parks Canada safety signs and closures.

Wickaninnish Beach and Kwisitis Visitor Centre

The Kwisitis Visitor Centre area is a good choice if you want a safer, more comfortable storm watching spot. The viewing deck gives you a strong view of the beach and waves.

This is also a good place to learn about the area’s natural history, wildlife, and local First Nations culture.

Best for: Viewing deck, learning, families, safer storm views

Good to know. This is a good option when the beach itself feels too rough.

Tonquin Beach

Tonquin Beach is smaller and more sheltered than the larger surf beaches. It is reached by a forest trail from town.

This beach can be beautiful in stormy weather, but the trail may be wet and slippery. It is better for a short visit than a long storm watching session.

Best for: Short walk, forest trail, quiet views

Good to know. Wear proper shoes because the trail can be muddy.

Pettinger Point

Accessible via a trail from Cox Bay, this headland offers a spectacular vantage point where waves explode into white spray against the cliffs.

Oceanfront Resorts and Cabins

One of the best ways to storm watch in Tofino is from inside. Many oceanfront hotels, cabins, and resorts have large windows facing the water.

This is perfect if the weather is too rough to be outside. You can watch the storm with a warm drink, a blanket, and dry socks.

The Wickaninnish Inn is the most iconic destination for this experience, specifically built on a rocky headland to withstand and showcase the Pacific's power.
