7-Day Vancouver Island Hiking Trip

From Coastal Bluffs to Forest Falls

Length
7 days
Towns
Victoria, Sooke, Ucluelet, Campbell River
Best for
Walkers

Vancouver Island has plenty of day hikes, from rocky shore paths to rainforest boardwalks. A week gives you time to see a few different areas and still sleep in a bed each night. This trip suits walkers who enjoy spending a few hours outside and choosing their distance as they go. The drives between the main hiking areas give your legs a break. Check trail conditions, tides, and daylight before you leave, and have a shorter walk in mind for wet or windy days.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria coast

    Walk from Beacon Hill Park toward Dallas Road and turn back when you’re ready. Stay in Victoria.

  2. Day 2: East Sooke

    Choose a shorter out-and-back on open trails from Aylard Farm in East Sooke Regional Park. Skip rough shoreline paths in poor weather and check sunset before you set off.

  3. Day 3: Cathedral Grove to Ucluelet

    Stop for an old-growth walk if parking and trails are open. Drive on to Ucluelet and rest your legs.

  4. Day 4: Wild Pacific Trail

    Walk the Lighthouse Loop first, then add part of the Brown’s Beach section if you want more distance.

  5. Day 5: Pacific Rim

    Link a rainforest loop with a separate Long Beach walk. Check tides and surf before going near the water and stay off exposed rocks.

  6. Day 6: Transfer to Campbell River

    Drive back across the Island. If you arrive early, stretch your legs along the Campbell River waterfront.

  7. Day 7: Elk Falls

    Hike near Elk Falls on open trails, then head south to Nanaimo if taking a ferry. Keep the hike short if you have a fixed departure.

Recommended stops

  • East Sooke Regional ParkPick an open out-and-back from Aylard Farm.
  • Rainforest Trail and Long BeachUse one day for two short walks in different settings.
  • Elk FallsWalk an open route to a viewpoint near Campbell River.

Tips for this trip

  • Carry an offline map, water, rain gear, and a light. Tell someone where you’re going and when you’ll be back.
  • Check park notices and tides before coastal hikes. Turn back if waves, wind, or fading light affect your route.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include the West Coast Trail?

This route uses day hikes. An overnight West Coast Trail trek is a separate trip with reservations, gear, and tide planning.

Which trails are good in rain?

Choose short open trails that suit the conditions. High winds, flooding, and falling branches can change what’s safe even in the forest.

Do you need hiking boots?

Wear shoes with good grip that suit wet and uneven paths. Sturdy hiking footwear helps on rougher trails.

Can families do this hiking trip?

Yes. Shorten the outings and use the Lighthouse Loop, Victoria waterfront, and open rainforest paths as the main walks.

Which hike should you skip if time is short?

Keep Elk Falls brief or skip it if you have an early ferry on the last day.
