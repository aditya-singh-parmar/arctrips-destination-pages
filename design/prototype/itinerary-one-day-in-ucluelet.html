One Day in Ucluelet

Ucluelet’s Lighthouse and Harbor

Length
1 day
Towns
Ucluelet

Ucluelet is a good place to slow down. You can watch the waves from the bluffs, then be back at the harbor for lunch without having to cross town all day. One day is plenty for a first visit if you like short walks and time to stop at the viewpoints. Families can keep the walks short, too. If it’s been windy, check trail notices before heading out and give the water plenty of space.

The plan

  1. Morning

    Start on the Lighthouse Loop of the Wild Pacific Trail. Pause at the marked viewpoints near Amphitrite Point. The loop has plenty of places to stop, so leave time to watch the waves from the trail.

  2. Afternoon

    Have lunch by the harbor and check whether the Ucluelet Aquarium is open. If you want more time outside, try the Brown’s Beach to Ancient Cedars section of the Wild Pacific Trail. Drive between trail sections if you want to save walking time.

  3. Evening

    Take a short walk at Big Beach if conditions are calm, then head into town for dinner. When the sea is rough, keep to viewpoints and stay off wet rocks and logs.

Recommended stops

  • Amphitrite PointStop at the marked Lighthouse Loop viewpoints.
  • Ucluelet AquariumMake it your harbor stop if open, especially if you’re traveling with children.
  • Brown’s BeachStart here for a second Wild Pacific Trail walk and turn back when you’re ready.

Tips for this trip

  • Use signed viewpoints and stay clear of rocks and logs when waves are rough.
  • The Wild Pacific Trail has separate sections. Drive between them if your group wants to save its energy for the views.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ucluelet worth visiting for one day?

Yes. The Lighthouse Loop and harbor give you a feel for town without a long drive between stops.

Can you see Tofino and Ucluelet in one day?

Yes, with one main stop in each town. Start in Ucluelet, stop at Long Beach, and finish in Tofino if you’re staying there.

Which part of the Wild Pacific Trail is best for a first visit?

Start with the Lighthouse Loop. It’s easy to follow and has several places to pause.

Is the Wild Pacific Trail free?

Yes. You can walk the public trail without paying for admission.

What can you do in Ucluelet if it rains?

Visit the aquarium when it’s open or have a long lunch by the harbor. Save the viewpoints for when conditions improve.
