Victoria to Ucluelet: 7-Day Island Adventure

Island Roads to Ucluelet

Length
7 days
Towns
Victoria, Ucluelet, Cowichan Valley, Port Alberni, Tofino, Parksville and 2 more

Ucluelet is a good place to spend a few mornings by the sea, especially if you like a quiet harbor and views from the bluffs. A week from Victoria gives you room to go slowly through the Cowichan Valley and see a little more of the east side on the way back. This is a good trip for couples or friends who like short walks and don’t want to change rooms every night. Check Highway 4 before leaving and plan the last night around your trip home.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria

    Walk the Inner Harbour, Chinatown, and James Bay. Stay downtown.

  2. Day 2: Cowichan Valley

    Cross the Malahat, have lunch in Cowichan Bay, and take a short walk in Duncan. Stay near Duncan or Cowichan Bay.

  3. Day 3: Port Alberni to Ucluelet

    Check Highway 4 before leaving. Stop for a meal in Port Alberni and reach Ucluelet with enough daylight for the harbor. Stay three nights.

  4. Day 4: Wild Pacific Trail

    Walk the Lighthouse Loop, then split the afternoon between the aquarium if open and the viewpoints near Brown’s Beach.

  5. Day 5: Long Beach and Tofino

    Visit Long Beach and an open rainforest loop, then spend the rest of the day in Tofino. Drive back to Ucluelet for dinner.

  6. Day 6: Parksville or Qualicum Beach

    Head east, with a Cathedral Grove stop if parking is available. Spend the evening near the water in Parksville or Qualicum Beach.

  7. Day 7: Nanaimo

    Walk the harbor if you have time before your ferry. If flying from Victoria, skip downtown Nanaimo and allow extra time to drive south.

Recommended stops

  • Cowichan BayBreak up the northbound drive with a meal by the harbor.
  • Wild Pacific TrailSpend time at both the Lighthouse Loop and Brown’s Beach viewpoints.
  • Long BeachAdd this on the day you drive to Tofino.

Tips for this trip

  • Get groceries for your first night before the last stretch to Ucluelet.
  • Stay near Parksville or Qualicum Beach on the way out if you have an early Nanaimo ferry.

Frequently asked questions

Can you drive Victoria to Ucluelet in one day?

Yes. The overnight stop gives time for the Cowichan Valley and keeps the road day easier.

Which town is better for a quiet stay?

Ucluelet has harbor and oceanfront stays close to the trail. Pick a room near the water if the view matters to you.

Can you day-trip to Tofino from Ucluelet?

Yes. Stop at Long Beach along the way if time and weather allow.

Where should you stay for walks in Ucluelet?

Choose a place near the Lighthouse Loop for an early bluff walk or near the harbor for meals on foot.

Can you finish the trip in Victoria?

Yes. Leave more road time on Day 7 or add a final night there before a morning flight.
