Plan your trip to Tofino

When to come, how many days to give it, and the guides worth reading first. Any month works for a different reason: July and August are the busiest, spring and fall are quieter, and winter is storm season with the fewest people.

Best time to visit Itineraries Guides

Best time to visit

Choose by what you want to do and how many people you want to share it with, then dress for the season you pick.

Conditions by month
Summer
Busiest. July and August fill the beaches, the tours and the rooms. The long days suit beach time, paddling and wildlife tours.
Fall
Quieter. Fewer crowds and a more relaxed pace, with whale watching still running into October.
Winter
Quietest. Storm season, November to February, when the swell is biggest and the coast is at its most dramatic.
Spring
Building. Gray whales pass on their way north from March, and the Pacific Rim Whale Festival fills the towns.
