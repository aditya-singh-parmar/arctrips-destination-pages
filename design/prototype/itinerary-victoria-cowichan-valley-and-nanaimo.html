Suggested itinerary

Victoria, Cowichan Valley, and Nanaimo

Gardens and Harbor Towns of Vancouver Island

Length
4 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Nanaimo, Ladysmith
Best for
Travelers who like a gentle pace and short drives

You can drive from Victoria to Nanaimo in part of a day, but it’s the smaller places along the way that make this trip worth stretching out. The Cowichan Valley is a good place to linger over lunch, and the gardens and waterfront walks give you easy ways to spend the rest of the time. Four days suits travelers who like a gentle pace and short drives. Check opening days for your favorite stops before you settle on dates.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria’s old town

    Walk from the Inner Harbour to Chinatown. Try the Royal BC Museum in the afternoon or walk through James Bay to Beacon Hill Park. Stay downtown.

  2. Day 2: Butchart Gardens to the Cowichan Valley

    Visit the gardens in the morning. Cross the Malahat after lunch and settle in near Duncan or Cowichan Bay. Keep the evening free for a local dinner.

  3. Day 3: Cowichan Bay and Duncan

    Walk the bay’s main street, then have lunch by the water. Head to Duncan to see the downtown totem poles, or pick the BC Forest Discovery Centre if it’s open and your group wants a longer stop. Sleep in the valley again.

  4. Day 4: Ladysmith and Nanaimo

    Pause at Transfer Beach, then follow the highway to Nanaimo. Have lunch downtown and take a walk on the Harbourfront Walkway before your ferry or check-in.

Recommended stops

  • Butchart GardensMake this your main stop before driving over the Malahat.
  • Cowichan Bay and DuncanPair a waterfront lunch with the downtown totem poles.
  • Transfer BeachBreak up the last drive with a short walk in Ladysmith.

Tips for this trip

  • Book a valley stay for both nights to keep Day 3 easy.
  • Check Forest Discovery Centre hours before you plan the Duncan afternoon around it.

Frequently asked questions

Can you drive this route in one day?

Yes, but four days leaves time to walk and eat in each place. With two days, skip the gardens or one valley stop.

Where is the best place to stay in the Cowichan Valley?

Cowichan Bay works for a quiet waterfront stay. Duncan is handy for the highway and services.

What can you do in Nanaimo before a ferry?

Try a Nanaimo bar downtown and walk a short part of the Harbourfront Walkway if time allows.

Where can you stop with children?

Try Beacon Hill Park, the BC Forest Discovery Centre when open, or Transfer Beach.

Which way should you drive the route?

Start near the ferry or airport that suits your plans. Reverse the daily order if you arrive in Nanaimo.
