Three Days on Vancouver Island

An Easy Island Escape From Victoria to Nanaimo

Length
3 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Ladysmith, Nanaimo
Best for
A long weekend or a first Island trip

Vancouver Island is bigger than it looks on a map. With three days, the east side is a good choice if you like harbor walks and small towns and want to spend more time out of the car. Victoria and Nanaimo make useful start and end points, with the Cowichan Valley in between. This works well for a long weekend or a first Island trip. If you’re bringing a car on the ferry, check both terminal locations when you book.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria

    Start at the Inner Harbour, walk to Chinatown for lunch, then head south to Beacon Hill Park. If you’re coming in on a late ferry, keep the afternoon to downtown and save the park for another visit. Sleep in Victoria.

  2. Day 2: Cowichan Valley

    Drive over the Malahat and go to Cowichan Bay for a walk along the village’s waterfront. Pick up lunch there, then head to Duncan for the downtown totem poles or check the BC Forest Discovery Centre’s opening hours. Sleep in the Duncan area.

  3. Day 3: Ladysmith and Nanaimo

    Walk Transfer Beach in Ladysmith, then follow the highway north to Nanaimo. Park downtown, try a Nanaimo bar, and walk the Harbourfront Walkway. Leave enough time to reach the terminal on your reservation.

Recommended stops

  • Inner HarbourKeep the first day’s sights close together in Victoria.
  • Cowichan BayWalk the village waterfront and stay for lunch.
  • Nanaimo Harbourfront WalkwayFinish here before catching your ferry if time allows.

Tips for this trip

  • Stay near Duncan on the second night so you can reach Ladysmith without an early start.
  • Double-check your Nanaimo terminal. Departure Bay and Duke Point are in different places.

Frequently asked questions

Is three days enough for Vancouver Island?

It works for one part of the Island. Add more nights if Tofino or Ucluelet is a must.

Which ferries fit this route?

From the mainland, use Tsawwassen to Swartz Bay to start. From Nanaimo, pick Departure Bay for Horseshoe Bay or Duke Point for Tsawwassen.

What’s a good stop between Duncan and Nanaimo?

Transfer Beach in Ladysmith makes an easy leg stretch or picnic stop.

Can you add Butchart Gardens to three days?

Yes. Give it most of Day 1 and keep downtown Victoria to an evening harbor walk.

Do you need a car for this Island route?

A car makes it easier to stop in the smaller communities and reach your chosen ferry terminal.
