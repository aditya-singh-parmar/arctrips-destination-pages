Ten days from Victoria to Ucluelet

Length
10 days
Towns
Victoria, Chemainus, Nanaimo, Parksville, Campbell River, Tofino and 1 more

Two days in Victoria, then Chemainus, Nanaimo, Parksville, two days in Campbell River, and three days on the west coast.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria landmarks

    • Inner Harbour The heart of Victoria, framed by the Neo-baroque Parliament Buildings and the Fairmont Empress Hotel.
    • BC Parliament Buildings Victoria's premier architectural landmark: a grand Neo-baroque complex with a copper dome, on the Inner Harbour.
    • Royal BC Museum Walk through a replica 19th-century town, stand beneath a towering woolly mammoth and explore Indigenous cultures. Across from the Parliament Buildings.

  2. Day 2: Victoria parks and history

    • Beacon Hill Park A 200-acre urban park of Garry Oak meadows, floral gardens and rugged coastline.
    • Craigdarroch Castle A bonanza castle built by coal baron Robert Dunsmuir between 1887 and 1890, with 39 rooms, intricate woodwork and fine stained glass.
    • Fisherman's Wharf Colourful houseboats, fresh seafood kiosks and working fishing vessels in a living neighborhood on the water.

  3. Day 3: Chemainus

    • Chemainus Murals Once a struggling lumber town, now a walkable outdoor gallery of murals telling the region's Indigenous, forestry and community history.
    • Chemainus Theatre Festival A neo-classical building housing professional dinner-and-a-show theatre, anchoring the town's arts district.
    • Waterwheel Park The civic and horticultural heart of the town, named for a replica of the 19th-century waterwheel that once powered the local economy.

  4. Day 4: Nanaimo

    • Saysutshun (Newcastle Island Marine Provincial Park) An island park reached only by water, a few hundred meters off Nanaimo harbor, with sandstone cliffs, forest trails and Snuneymuxw First Nation history.
    • Harbourfront Walkway A scenic multi-use shoreline path connecting downtown with the harbor and Saysutshun (Newcastle Island).
    • Old City Quarter A revitalized historic district a few blocks uphill from the waterfront, with colorful storefronts, boutiques and locally owned eateries.
    • Nanaimo Bar Trail A self-guided tour of more than 30 stops, each with its own take on the no-bake dessert.

  5. Day 5: the Parksville and Qualicum area

    • Rathtrevor Beach Provincial Park Massive tidal range and ancient Douglas fir forests, with a woodland sanctuary that opens onto a vast sandy beach.
    • Qualicum A long, curved shoreline with panoramic views of the Coast Mountains, calmer than the bustling park in Parksville.
    • Old Country Market, Coombs The Old Country Market, famous for its goats on the roof, is a complex of eclectic shops and international eateries.

  6. Day 6: Campbell River waterfront and falls

    • Elk Falls Provincial Park Ten minutes from downtown: massive trees, a rushing river and a suspension bridge over a thundering waterfall.
    • Discovery Fishing Pier Canada's first saltwater fishing pier, stretching 180 meters into Discovery Passage.
    • Rotary Seawalk A paved 13-kilometer pathway along the ocean, connecting the south of the city to downtown.

  7. Day 7: Quadra Island and Ripple Rock

    • Quadra Island A 10-minute ferry ride from downtown: the largest Discovery Island, known for artists, hidden beaches and deep Indigenous history. Worth a full day.
    • Ripple Rock Trail An 8-kilometer return trail to a high cliff over Seymour Narrows, where the water moves as fast as a river.

  8. Day 8: Cathedral Grove to Tofino

    • Cathedral Grove Giant Douglas firs over 800 years old, on Highway 4 between Qualicum and Port Alberni.
    • Chesterman Beach A 2.7-kilometer stretch of white sand shaped like a W. Walk it, or take a beginner surf lesson on the sandy bottom.
    • Tonquin Beach A sunset-facing cove reached by an 800-meter gravel path with viewing platforms. Calmer water than the surf beaches.

  9. Day 9: Hot Springs Cove

    • Hot Springs Cove Reached only by water or air from Tofino: a 2-kilometer cedar boardwalk to five natural pools. A typical excursion lasts 6 to 7 hours.

  10. Day 10: Ucluelet

    • Wild Pacific Trail, Lighthouse Loop The 2.6-kilometer Lighthouse Loop takes about 45 minutes along black volcanic rock and leads to Amphitrite Point Lighthouse.
    • Amphitrite Point Lighthouse An active beacon on the southern tip of the peninsula, good for spotting migrating whales in spring and sea lions on the offshore rocks.
    • Ucluelet Aquarium A catch-and-release aquarium at the foot of Main Street with touch tanks. Fully indoor and seasonal, typically March through early December.
