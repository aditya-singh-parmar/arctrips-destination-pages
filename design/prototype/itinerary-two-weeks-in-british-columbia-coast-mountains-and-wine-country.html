Two Weeks in British Columbia: Coast, Mountains, and Wine Country

The Pacific Coast, High Peaks, and Vineyard Trail

Length
14 days
Towns
Vancouver, Squamish, Whistler, Kelowna, Penticton, Oliver and 1 more
Best for
Couples or friends

BC feels different on the waterfront, in a mountain town, and by an Okanagan vineyard. Two weeks gives you time to get to know each one and still leave a few afternoons free. This mainland loop is a good fit for couples or friends who enjoy a morning walk, a good meal, and a comfortable place to spend a few nights. Keep tasting stops close together, arrange rides on wine days, and allow a full day for the drive back west.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vancouver arrival

    Walk the waterfront near your hotel and eat in the West End.

  2. Day 2: Stanley Park

    Spend the morning on the Seawall, then go to English Bay for a late lunch and beach walk.

  3. Day 3: North Shore

    Ride the SeaBus to Lonsdale Quay and spend the afternoon at Lynn Canyon if open, or at the Shipyards.

  4. Day 4: Squamish

    Pick up your car, drive north, and visit Shannon Falls. Stay in Squamish for the evening.

  5. Day 5: Whistler

    Make the short transfer to Whistler. Explore the Village and visit the Squamish Lil’wat Cultural Centre if open.

  6. Day 6: Whistler outdoors

    Choose sightseeing lifts, a ski day, or a Valley Trail outing.

  7. Day 7: Drive to Kelowna

    Give the drive most of the day. Check DriveBC before deciding which route to take.

  8. Day 8: Myra Canyon

    Walk or ride the trestles if conditions allow. Take the afternoon off by Okanagan Lake.

  9. Day 9: West Kelowna

    Book a tasting and lunch with a sober ride. Spend the evening back downtown.

  10. Day 10: Peachland to Penticton

    Stop for coffee by the water in Peachland and walk near Okanagan Beach in Penticton.

  11. Day 11: Naramata Bench

    Walk a short rail trail section early, then book a winery lunch or explore Naramata village.

  12. Day 12: Oliver to Osoyoos

    Visit the Nk’Mip Desert Cultural Centre if open. Stay in Osoyoos for a quiet evening by the lake.

  13. Day 13: Return west

    Drive toward Vancouver via Highway 3 if conditions suit. Break for lunch along the way and stay on the mainland near your next stop.

  14. Day 14: False Creek or departure

    Spend any extra time at Granville Island or Olympic Village. Allow time for car return before your flight.

Recommended stops

  • The Shipyards and Lynn CanyonPair an inlet crossing with a North Shore walk.
  • Squamish and WhistlerSplit the mountain leg between highway stops and one full day in Whistler.
  • Naramata Bench and OsoyoosKeep two days for the southern wine country and the lake.

Tips for this trip

  • Book winery lunches and sober rides before filling in the rest of your Okanagan days.
  • Give Osoyoos its own night so you can start the return drive rested.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time for wine country?

Summer suits lake days. Early fall can be a good fit for tastings and harvest-season visits. Ask each winery about hours and bookings.

Do you need a car the whole two weeks?

No. Pick it up when you leave Vancouver on Day 4 and organize sober transport on tasting days.

Does the route require a ferry?

You won’t need a vehicle ferry. The SeaBus on the Vancouver day is a short passenger crossing.

Where should you spend extra time if you like wine?

Use more nights in Penticton to be close to the Naramata Bench, or add a night near Oliver for the south end.

Can families follow the same itinerary?

Yes. Use the tasting blocks for rail trails, public beaches, and a visit to the Nk’Mip Desert Cultural Centre if open.
