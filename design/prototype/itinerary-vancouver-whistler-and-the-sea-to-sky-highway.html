Vancouver, Whistler, and the Sea-to-Sky Highway

The Sea-to-Sky Scenic Drive

Length
4 days
Towns
Vancouver, Whistler, Squamish
Best for
Travelers who like a few short walks along a scenic drive

The Sea-to-Sky Highway is worth giving time to even if Whistler is the main reason you’re going. The views over Howe Sound have a different feel from the mountains up near the Village, and Squamish is a handy place to get out of the car. This four-day trip suits travelers who like a few short walks along a scenic drive. Allow a separate day for Whistler itself, and check the weather before paying for a gondola ride.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vancouver waterfront

    Walk the Seawall near Coal Harbour and spend the afternoon at Granville Island. Do grocery shopping or pick up a rental car before your northbound drive.

  2. Day 2: Howe Sound and Squamish

    Drive north on Highway 99. Stop at Shannon Falls, then have lunch in Squamish. Choose the Sea to Sky Gondola if views are clear, or a longer lunch and a downtown walk if clouds are low. Continue to Whistler.

  3. Day 3: Whistler

    Choose sightseeing lifts, a ski day, or a Valley Trail outing based on the season. Spend the evening on foot in the Village.

  4. Day 4: Southbound with one last stop

    Get breakfast in Whistler, then pause in Brackendale or at a designated Howe Sound viewpoint if weather and time allow. Head straight to Vancouver if you have an onward booking.

Recommended stops

  • Shannon FallsTake a short walk to the falls near Squamish.
  • Sea to Sky GondolaUse this as your main Squamish outing if the views are clear.
  • Whistler VillageFinish your mountain day within walking distance of dinner.

Tips for this trip

  • Stop for photos only in designated areas.
  • Check winter tire rules and road reports if traveling in colder months.

Frequently asked questions

Which stops are best on the Sea-to-Sky Highway?

Shannon Falls offers a short walk. Squamish is good for a meal and a longer stop. Pick the gondola if you want an afternoon activity.

Can you drive the route in winter?

Yes when it’s open and conditions suit your vehicle and skills. Check DriveBC and posted winter tire rules before leaving.

Can you visit Shannon Falls and the gondola in one day?

Yes. Give the gondola most of the time and keep the waterfall stop short.

Do you need two nights in Whistler for this route?

Two nights give you one full day in town. Keep it to one if the highway stops are your main reason for going.

Where should you stay if you want less driving at night?

Book near Whistler Village or Upper Village so you can reach dinner on foot.
