5-Day Romantic Vancouver Island Getaway

Harbor Nights and Oceanfront Mornings

Length
5 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley

Victoria and the west coast make a good pair for a few days away together. In town, you can walk to dinner and spend the evening by the harbor. On the coast, a room with a view is worth having, especially if you like slow mornings or get a rainy day. Five days gives couples time to enjoy both, with a day for the drive between them. If the view matters to you, ask which rooms face the water before booking.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria’s Inner Harbour

    Check in and spend the late afternoon along the harbor. Book dinner nearby so the first evening stays easy.

  2. Day 2: Gardens and James Bay

    Visit Butchart Gardens in the morning, then return to Victoria for lunch. Walk through James Bay toward Dallas Road if the weather’s good. Keep the evening open for a slow dinner.

  3. Day 3: Cowichan Bay and the coast

    Head north over the Malahat. Stop in Cowichan Bay for lunch if you leave early, then continue through Port Alberni to your oceanfront stay. Keep dinner near your room after the long drive.

  4. Day 4: One beach and one trail

    Start with the Lighthouse Loop if staying in Ucluelet or Chesterman Beach if staying near Tofino. Pick a second stop at Long Beach only if the surf and weather suit. Book dinner in your base town.

  5. Day 5: Slow morning and return

    Have breakfast with a view, then drive east. If you’re taking a ferry from Nanaimo, leave time for the road and check-in before adding stops.

Recommended stops

  • Inner HarbourTake a sunset walk before dinner if the weather holds.
  • Butchart GardensGive the garden paths a full morning.
  • Chesterman Beach or the Lighthouse LoopChoose the walk closest to your oceanfront stay.

Tips for this trip

  • Ask which rooms face the water before you book and check winter dinner hours.
  • Plan dinner close to your room on the long drive day, and keep the last morning free of timed stops.

Frequently asked questions

Where is a good place to stay for a romantic weekend?

Ucluelet suits a quiet oceanfront stay and rocky bluff walks. Tofino suits sandy beach walks and dinner in town.

Is winter a good time for this trip?

It can be, especially if you want to watch storms from your room or a safe viewpoint. Check road and trail conditions and book dinner ahead where hours are limited.

Can you do this getaway with just one hotel change?

Yes. Spend the first two nights in Victoria and the last two in the same oceanfront room near Tofino or Ucluelet.

Can you watch a winter storm from your room?

You can if the room faces open water and has a clear view. Confirm the view with the property before booking.

What’s a good way to break up the drive from Victoria?

Stop in Cowichan Bay for a meal if you start early. Port Alberni is a useful stop for fuel and food.
