Two Days in Vancouver

Vancouver’s Seawall and the North Shore

Length
2 days
Towns
Vancouver, North Vancouver

Two days in Vancouver lets you see how close the city is to the forest. You can spend one day around the downtown waterfront and take the SeaBus across Burrard Inlet the next. It’s a good trip for first-time visitors who want to get around without a car. Pick one main North Shore stop so you have time to enjoy it, and check how you’ll get from the SeaBus to the trail or bridge you want to visit.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Stanley Park and False Creek

    Start on the Stanley Park Seawall from Coal Harbour. Walk to Brockton Point or rent a bike for more of the park. Head to Granville Island for lunch and a walk through the market. Finish with dinner on Main Street or in the West End, depending on where you’re staying.

  2. Day 2: North Vancouver

    Take the SeaBus from Waterfront Station to Lonsdale Quay. Continue by transit to Lynn Canyon for forest paths and the suspension bridge if open, or choose the paid Capilano Suspension Bridge Park. Give one stop most of the afternoon. Back at the quay, have dinner near the Shipyards and watch the city lights from across the inlet.

Recommended stops

  • Stanley Park SeawallStart from Coal Harbour and turn back whenever you like.
  • Lynn Canyon or CapilanoPick one bridge and check access before crossing the inlet.
  • The ShipyardsEnd the second day near Lonsdale Quay for dinner and skyline views.

Tips for this trip

  • Check your North Shore bus route before the SeaBus ride.
  • Leave dinner close to your last stop to cut travel time at the end of each day.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do in Vancouver in two days without a car?

This route works by foot and transit. Check the onward bus for your North Shore stop before boarding the SeaBus.

What if it rains?

Keep the market and add the Museum of Vancouver or Vancouver Art Gallery. Check opening days before you go.

Which suspension bridge should you visit?

Pick Lynn Canyon for a forest walk when paths are open. Choose Capilano if you want a paid attraction with more planned stops.

Where can you see the Vancouver skyline?

Head to the waterfront near the Shipyards and look back across Burrard Inlet.

How much time should you spend at Granville Island?

Leave time for lunch and a walk around the shops. An hour or two works for most visitors.
