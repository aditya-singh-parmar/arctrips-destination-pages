Five Days in the Okanagan

Five Days of Lakes and Vineyard Views

Length
5 days
Towns
Okanagan, Kelowna, Penticton, Oliver, Osoyoos
Best for
Couples or families

The Okanagan is a good place to leave a few afternoons open. You might feel like a swim, a long lunch, or a walk between the vines once you see what the day is like. Five days works well for couples or families who want time by the lake and a choice of things to do. Two bases keep the driving down. In hot weather, do the exposed walks early and save the middle of the day for shade.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Kelowna waterfront

    Check in downtown and walk from Waterfront Park to City Park. Pick a patio for dinner if it’s warm enough.

  2. Day 2: Myra Canyon and West Kelowna

    Walk or bike the trestles from Myra Station if conditions are good. After lunch, choose one West Kelowna winery with a sober driver, or spend the rest of the day by the lake.

  3. Day 3: Peachland to Penticton

    Stop in Peachland for coffee and a waterfront walk. In Penticton, spend the afternoon at Okanagan Beach and have dinner downtown. Stay two nights.

  4. Day 4: Naramata Bench

    Walk part of the Kettle Valley Rail Trail in the morning. Book a winery lunch or tasting with a sober ride, or take a picnic down to Naramata village.

  5. Day 5: Oliver and Osoyoos

    Head south for a walk by Osoyoos Lake and visit the Nk’Mip Desert Cultural Centre if open. If you have a flight out of Kelowna, leave the south end for another trip or add a night.

Recommended stops

  • Myra CanyonWalk the trestles early or book bikes in season.
  • Okanagan BeachGive the Penticton waterfront a full afternoon.
  • Naramata BenchPick a winery lunch, a rail trail walk, or a picnic in the village.

Tips for this trip

  • Book sober transport for tasting days before setting your times.
  • Try a shorter morning walk if it’s hot and leave the middle of the day for shade.

Frequently asked questions

What’s the best base for five days?

Kelowna and Penticton split the driving well. Pick Penticton alone if you care most about the south end.

What can families do instead of wine tasting?

Plan for beaches, the trestles, fruit stands in season, and shorter stretches of rail trail.

Can you visit Osoyoos as a day trip from Penticton?

Yes. Leave early enough to stop in Oliver or visit the cultural center if open.

Is Okanagan Lake or Skaha Lake better for a beach day?

Choose Okanagan Beach to stay near downtown Penticton. Pick Skaha Lake Park for a day at the south end of town.

Can you do this trip with one hotel?

Yes, especially from Penticton. Count on more driving for the Kelowna and Myra Canyon stops.
