Seven days of Vancouver Island mountain biking

Length
7 days
Towns
Victoria, Nanaimo, Parksville, Campbell River

Ride Victoria, Cowichan, Nanaimo, Parksville, Cumberland, Campbell River and Mount Washington for the full biking road trip.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria

    • Victoria and Hartland City-access riding at Hartland Mountain Bike Park, rocky, rooty and technical. Not a soft beginner path.

  2. Day 2: Cowichan Valley

    • Cowichan Valley A region of several riding zones: Cobble Hill for easier riding, Mount Tzouhalem for variety, Maple Mountain for technical forest and Mount Prevost for advanced downhill.

  3. Day 3: Nanaimo

    • Nanaimo Technical riding with rocky sections, roots, climbs and ridgelines across several zones, including Doumont, Westwood, South Benson and Extension Ridge.

  4. Day 4: Parksville and Hammerfest

    • Parksville Qualicum and Hammerfest Hammerfest trail network near Englishman River Falls Provincial Park: old-school singletrack, technical ridges, flow, bridges and boardwalks.

  5. Day 5: Cumberland

    • Cumberland and Comox Valley Often considered the heart of Vancouver Island mountain biking: forest singletrack, loam, flow trails and technical descents beside a bike-town village.

  6. Day 6: Campbell River

    • Campbell River and Snowden Forest Snowden Demonstration Forest has a large singletrack network. A slower, more spacious trip with fewer crowds, and other outdoor options nearby.

  7. Day 7: Mount Washington

    • Mount Washington Resort-style summer riding in an alpine setting with cooler air and views. Check current summer operations before going.

Tips for this trip

  • Spring through fall. Fall is one of the best seasons, and spring dirt can be very good but wet.
