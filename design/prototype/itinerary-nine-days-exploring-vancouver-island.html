Nine Days Exploring Vancouver Island

South Island, Pacific Rim, and Quadra

Length
9 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Parksville, Ucluelet, Tofino, Campbell River and 1 more
Best for
Travelers

Nine days gives you room to settle into a few parts of Vancouver Island. The west coast is where you’ll want extra time, especially if a change in the weather has you moving beach walks around. The stretch near Campbell River adds quieter shoreline and the chance to spend a day on Quadra Island. This trip suits travelers who like short hikes, small towns, and a few unscheduled afternoons. Check the Quadra ferry times before deciding how much to fit in.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria

    Walk the Inner Harbour and Chinatown, then follow the waterfront toward James Bay.

  2. Day 2: Saanich Peninsula

    Visit Butchart Gardens and have lunch in Sidney. Return to Victoria for dinner.

  3. Day 3: Cowichan Valley to Parksville

    Stop for lunch in Cowichan Bay and walk by the water in Ladysmith. Spend the night near Parksville.

  4. Day 4: Pacific Rim coast

    Stop at Cathedral Grove if parking allows. Take a road break in Port Alberni, then continue to Ucluelet for the night.

  5. Day 5: Ucluelet

    Walk the Lighthouse Loop and have lunch by the harbor. Spend the afternoon on the Brown’s Beach section or at the aquarium when open.

  6. Day 6: Tofino and Long Beach

    Walk Chesterman Beach and have lunch in Tofino. Stop at Long Beach on your way back to your west coast base.

  7. Day 7: Campbell River

    Drive back across Highway 4 and north to Campbell River. Have dinner near the waterfront and stay two nights.

  8. Day 8: Quadra Island or Elk Falls

    Take the short ferry to Quadra Island for a visit to Rebecca Spit if the schedule and weather suit. Or stay around Campbell River and walk at Elk Falls when trails are open.

  9. Day 9: Nanaimo

    Drive south to Nanaimo and take a mainland ferry or finish your trip by the harbor.

Recommended stops

  • Butchart GardensGive the Saanich Peninsula its own morning.
  • Ucluelet and TofinoUse separate days for the Lighthouse Loop and Chesterman Beach.
  • Rebecca SpitPlan a short walk if you make the Quadra Island crossing.

Tips for this trip

  • Reserve the main Island crossings early if you’re bringing a car on busy dates.
  • Fuel before Highway 4 and leave the coast in daylight if you can.

Frequently asked questions

Should you take your car to Quadra Island?

It helps if Rebecca Spit is your goal. Check the ferry and arrange transport before crossing on foot.

Can you add Port Hardy?

Yes with more days. On this route, staying near Campbell River gives you more time out of the car.

Is Quadra Island worth a day trip from Campbell River?

Yes if you want a quieter shoreline outing. Plan how you’ll get to Rebecca Spit from the ferry dock.

Which base needs the most nights?

The west coast gets three nights so you can visit both towns and have room to adjust for weather.

What can you do if you skip Quadra?

Try Elk Falls if trails are open, then have lunch and walk the Campbell River waterfront.
