Dinner, and the special one

01 to 05

Book these, especially in summer and at weekends.

01

Best forLocal seafood and foraged flavours

Dinner

Wolf in the Fog

Wolf in the Fog is one of the most famous restaurants in Tofino. It is a good choice for a special dinner, a date night, or a meal that feels very connected to the west coast.

The menu is inspired by Tofino’s ocean, forest, and shoreline. Expect seafood, local produce, foraged flavors, creative cocktails, and dishes made for sharing.

Good for special dinners, local seafood, cocktails, and 3 more.

Good to know. Book ahead if you can. This is one of the most popular restaurants in town.

DinnerOcean-view fine dining

The Pointe Restaurant

The Pointe Restaurant is one of the best places in Tofino for ocean-view dining. It is located at the Wickaninnish Inn, near Chesterman Beach, and is known for its wide Pacific Ocean views.

This is a good choice if you want a more polished dining experience. It works well for anniversaries, birthdays, romantic dinners, or a special meal during a Tofino trip.

Good for ocean views, fine dining, special occasions, and 3 more.

Good to know. This is one of the more upscale dining options in Tofino. Check dress expectations and book ahead.

03

Best forWaterfront casual dining

Dinner

Shelter Restaurant

Shelter Restaurant is a popular waterfront spot near Tofino Resort + Marina. It is a great choice if you want a relaxed meal with local seafood, burgers, bowls, and a cozy coastal feel.

Shelter works well for many types of travelers. It is casual enough for families but still feels like a proper sit-down dinner.

Good for waterfront dining, families, seafood, and 3 more.

Good to know. It can get busy, especially in summer and at dinner time.

04

Best forCreative local dining

Dinner

Ombré Restaurant

Ombré is a newer Tofino dining option connected to the team behind Wolf in the Fog. It has a more relaxed feel but still focuses on creative food and local flavor.

It is a good choice if you want something stylish, thoughtful, and a little different from the classic seafood spots.

Good for creative dining, small plates, date night, and 3 more.

Good to know. Check current hours and reservation options before going.

05

Best forLive-fire cooking

Dinner

ROAR Tofino

ROAR is located at Hotel Zed Tofino and is known for live-fire cooking. The food is warm, bold, and inspired by local ingredients and west coast stories.

This is a good choice if you want something fun and a little different. It is not just a simple seafood meal. The fire cooking gives the food a smoky, cozy feel.

Good for live-fire cooking, dinner with friends, local ingredients, and 3 more.

Good to know. ROAR is a good pick if you want a restaurant that feels modern but still relaxed.

Casual, quick, after the beach

06 to 08

No booking, sometimes a queue. This is where most of the town eats lunch.

CasualFish tacos and burritos

Tacofino Original Truck

Tacofino is one of the most famous food stops in Tofino. The original food truck is known for fish tacos, burritos, and west coast Mexican-style food.

This is a classic after-surf meal. It is casual, quick, and easy to love.

Good for fish tacos, burritos, lunch, and 3 more.

Good to know. Lines can be long in peak season. Go early or outside busy meal times if you can.

07

Best forFish and chips

Casual

Wildside Grill

Wildside Grill is a local favorite for fish and chips, seafood, burgers, and casual west coast comfort food.

It is a good choice after surfing, beach walking, or driving back from Pacific Rim National Park. The food is relaxed, filling, and easy to enjoy.

Good for fish and chips, seafood, burgers, and 3 more.

Good to know. It is located in the Live to Surf plaza area, which is handy if you are staying or exploring outside the village center.

08

Best forBurgers and bowls

Casual

Shed Tofino

Shed is a casual restaurant in downtown Tofino. It is known for burgers, bowls, drinks, and a relaxed west coast feel.

This is a good option if you want something easy but still fresh. It works well for lunch, dinner, families, and groups.

Good for burgers, bowls, casual dinner, and 3 more.

Good to know. Shed is a good “safe choice” when people in your group want different things.

Rhino Coffee House

Rhino Coffee House is one of the best-known coffee shops in Tofino. It is popular with locals and visitors for coffee, breakfast, sandwiches, bagels, and donuts.

This is a good place to start the day before surfing, hiking, kayaking, or driving to the beaches.

Tofitian Cafe is a long-time Tofino coffee spot. It has a local feel and is a good place for coffee, tea, and a slower start to the day.

It is a handy stop if you are near the surf shops and the Pacific Rim Highway area.

Good for coffee, tea, morning stops, and 3 more.

Driftwood Café is located at the Wickaninnish Inn near Chesterman Beach. It is a good choice for coffee, breakfast, brunch, or a light meal close to the beach.

It is also a nice option if you want something calmer and more scenic than a busy downtown cafe.

Good for coffee near Chesterman Beach, brunch, light meals, and 2 more.

Good to know. It is a nice stop before or after a walk on Chesterman Beach.

Before you go

Four things worth knowing about eating here

Tofino is a small, remote, popular town, and its restaurants behave like it.

Book dinner early

Reservations matter in summer, at weekends, and at the popular rooms all year. If you want one specific dinner on your trip, book it before you arrive.

Check the hours first

Some places close seasonally or change their hours without much notice. A quick check before you drive out saves the drive back.

Expect a queue at the casual places

Lines at the food trucks are longest at peak meal times in high season. Go early, go late, or bring a rain jacket for the wait.

Mix it up to spend less

Food here is expensive because everything arrives by one road. Food trucks, cafes and takeout for most meals, and one or two proper dinners, is how most people do it.

Questions

Asked before people book

Open the ones you need. Everything here is answered in the same words the guide uses.

What is the best restaurant in Tofino?

Many visitors consider Wolf in the Fog one of the best restaurants in Tofino because it focuses on local seafood, foraged ingredients, cocktails, and west coast flavor. The Pointe Restaurant is another top choice for fine dining and ocean views.

Do you need reservations in Tofino?

Yes, reservations are a good idea for dinner, especially in summer, on weekends, and at popular restaurants.

Is Tofino expensive for food?

Tofino can be expensive because it is remote and popular. You can save money by mixing food trucks, cafes, takeout, and a few special sit-down meals.

Are there vegetarian options in Tofino?

Yes. Many restaurants and cafes offer vegetarian options. Look at Shed, Tacofino, ROAR, Ombré, Basic Goodness Pizzeria, and local cafes.

What restaurants are good after surfing?

Tacofino, Wildside Grill, Shed, Rhino Coffee House, and Tofitian Cafe are great after a surf session.

What food is Tofino known for?

Fresh seafood above all: fish tacos, fish and chips, oysters, Dungeness crab, spot prawns and salmon, alongside local coffee, foraged ingredients and craft beer. You will also find Japanese and Korean flavours, Mexican-style tacos, burgers, pizza, vegetarian food, baked goods and gelato.

What does slow food mean in Tofino?

Food that respects the place: seafood from nearby waters, seasonal ingredients, local farms and producers, foraged mushrooms, greens and sea plants, small local businesses, menus that change with the season, and less waste. The Tofino Ucluelet Culinary Guild connects local restaurants and stores with Vancouver Island producers.
