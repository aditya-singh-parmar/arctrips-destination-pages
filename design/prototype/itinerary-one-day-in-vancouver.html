One Day in Vancouver

Vancouver by the Water

Length
1 day
Towns
Vancouver
Best for
First-time visitors

Vancouver is the kind of city where you can spend most of the day by the water and still feel like you’ve seen a lot. With one day, the waterfront around downtown and False Creek gives you an easy introduction to the city. It’s a good fit for first-time visitors who like to walk, browse a market, and leave time for a meal. The distances can sneak up on you, so use transit for the longer stretches if you want to save your feet.

The plan

  1. Morning

    Get coffee in the West End and enter Stanley Park near Coal Harbour. Walk a short stretch to the totem poles at Brockton Point, or rent a bike for a longer ride. Check posted directions and closures before setting out.

  2. Afternoon

    Head to Granville Island for the Public Market. Pick up lunch and sit outside by False Creek if the weather holds. A small passenger ferry is a fun way to cross the creek if the route fits. Check the last sailing if you plan to come back that way.

  3. Evening

    Walk through Gastown for dinner and a look at the old brick streets. If the sky is clear and you’d rather stay outside, head to English Bay for sunset and eat in the West End.

Recommended stops

  • Brockton PointMake the totem poles your turn-around point on a short Seawall walk.
  • Granville Island Public MarketPick up a few things for lunch and eat by False Creek if it’s dry.
  • English BayEnd here if sunset by the water sounds better than a second downtown walk.

Tips for this trip

  • Pick up a rental bike near the park if you plan to ride. Follow posted cycle directions and detours.
  • Give the market more time around lunch and check the last False Creek ferry before relying on it.

Frequently asked questions

Is one day enough for Vancouver?

It’s enough for Stanley Park, Granville Island, and one evening stop. Give the North Shore its own day.

Can you get around without a car?

Yes. Walk, take transit, or use a False Creek ferry. Check the small ferries’ fares separately.

Can you walk the whole Stanley Park Seawall in one day?

Yes. If you also want time for Granville Island and dinner downtown, walk a shorter stretch or rent a bike.

What can you do in Vancouver on a rainy day?

Start with the covered Public Market and spend the afternoon at the Vancouver Art Gallery if open.

Where should you stay for a short Vancouver visit?

The West End is handy for Stanley Park. Downtown near a transit stop makes it easier to reach your other stops.
