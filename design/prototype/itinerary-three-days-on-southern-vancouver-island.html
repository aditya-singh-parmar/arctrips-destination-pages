Three days on southern Vancouver Island

Length
3 days
Towns
Victoria, Sooke, Sidney

Victoria's harbor and museum, Sooke's river pools and beaches, then Butchart Gardens and the Sidney waterfront.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria harbor

    • Inner Harbour The heart of Victoria, framed by the Neo-baroque Parliament Buildings and the Fairmont Empress Hotel.
    • BC Parliament Buildings Victoria's premier architectural landmark: a grand Neo-baroque complex with a copper dome, on the Inner Harbour.
    • Royal BC Museum Walk through a replica 19th-century town, stand beneath a towering woolly mammoth and explore Indigenous cultures. Across from the Parliament Buildings.
    • Fisherman's Wharf Colourful houseboats, fresh seafood kiosks and working fishing vessels in a living neighborhood on the water.

  2. Day 2: Sooke coast

    • Sooke Potholes Provincial Park Deep, smooth rock pools on the Sooke River, carved from volcanic rock by boulders in swirling currents.
    • Whiffin Spit A 1.5-kilometer gravel bar into Sooke Harbour: a quick coastal walk with mountain and water views.
    • French Beach Provincial Park A long pebble-and-sand beach on the Juan de Fuca Strait, about 20 kilometers west of Sooke, popular with families.

  3. Day 3: Sidney and the Saanich Peninsula

    • The Butchart Gardens A 55-acre garden grown from a former limestone quarry, a National Historic Site of Canada transformed by Jennie Butchart from 1904.
    • Sidney Waterfront A flat, barrier-free 2.5-kilometer paved walkway with public art and views of the Gulf Islands and Mount Baker.
    • Beacon Avenue Canada's only Booktown, with independent shops and cafes along a tree-lined avenue sloping down to the Salish Sea.
