3-Day Okanagan Wine Country Getaway

A Toast to Three Wine Country Stops

Length
3 days
Towns
Okanagan, Kelowna, Oliver, Osoyoos
Best for
Couples or a small group

The Okanagan is a good place for a wine weekend, especially if you like to sit down for lunch and enjoy the view between tastings. Three days gives you time to see a few wine areas without filling your whole day with bookings. It works well for couples or a small group who can share a ride and like to taste at their own pace. Spend each day in one area, book a sober ride, and check whether your last tasting leaves enough time for the trip home.

The plan

  1. Day 1: West Kelowna

    Start with lunch and a booked tasting on the west side of the lake. Ask the winery if it offers a patio or other space with a lake view. Leave the evening for downtown Kelowna and stay there for the night.

  2. Day 2: Naramata Bench

    Drive south after breakfast. Meet your tour or sober driver in Penticton and spend the afternoon on the bench. If your first booking is late, walk by Okanagan Beach before lunch. Stay in Penticton so you can walk to dinner.

  3. Day 3: Oliver and Osoyoos

    Head south to Oliver for your final tasting. If you have the afternoon free, continue to Osoyoos for a walk by the lake. With an early flight from Kelowna, stay closer to the airport and move this day to a longer trip.

Recommended stops

  • West KelownaStart with a booked tasting and lunch on the west side of the lake.
  • Naramata BenchMeet your driver in Penticton and make this the main tasting day.
  • OliverPick one last winery, then walk by the lake in Osoyoos if time allows.

Tips for this trip

  • Ask about fees, food, and patio hours before booking.
  • Leave a gap between tastings for lunch, water, and travel.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need reservations for Okanagan wineries?

It depends on the winery and season. Book your priority stops ahead and ask about tasting fees and hours.

Can you do the wineries without driving?

Yes. Book a tour or private driver and confirm hotel pickup, especially outside the main towns.

When is a good season for an Okanagan wine trip?

Summer works for patio visits. Early fall can suit a harvest-season trip, with hours varying by winery.

How many wineries should you visit in one day?

Two booked stops give you time for lunch and the drive. Pick a third only if it’s nearby and your ride can fit it in.

Where can you stay near the Naramata Bench?

Penticton works for easy dinners and tour pickups. Naramata village can suit a quieter stay if your driver can pick you up there.
