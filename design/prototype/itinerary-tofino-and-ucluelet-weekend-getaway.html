Tofino and Ucluelet Weekend Getaway

A Weekend on the Wild Coast

Length
Weekend, Friday to Sunday
Towns
Tofino, Ucluelet

A weekend on Vancouver Island’s west coast is long enough for salty air, a couple of good walks, and dinner somewhere you can linger. Tofino is known for its wide beaches, while Ucluelet is where you’ll find rocky bluffs and a quieter harbor. Stay in one town for both nights, especially if you’re coming for a short break with your partner or friends. If you have an early ferry home, count back from check-in before making Sunday plans.

The plan

  1. Friday

    Make the drive your only big plan. Stop for groceries or dinner in Port Alberni if you’ll arrive late. Stock up on breakfast supplies so you can get outside early the next day.

  2. Saturday

    Give Tofino and the beaches a full day. Start at Chesterman Beach, have lunch in town, and stop at Long Beach or a Rainforest Trail loop in the afternoon. If you’re staying in Ucluelet, book dinner back in town so you can end the day near your room.

  3. Sunday

    Walk Ucluelet’s Lighthouse Loop and grab lunch by the harbor before heading home. If you have a ferry to catch from Nanaimo, build in time for Highway 4, fuel, and terminal check-in. Move the hike to Saturday if you have an early sailing.

Recommended stops

  • Chesterman BeachGive this the best beach weather on Saturday.
  • Long Beach or the Rainforest TrailPick one park stop on the drive between towns.
  • Lighthouse LoopSave this for Sunday morning if you have a late ferry.

Tips for this trip

  • Book Friday and Saturday at the same place so you can leave your bags unpacked.
  • Fuel in Port Alberni and check Highway 4 before each crossing.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to stay in Tofino or Ucluelet?

Pick the town where you want to spend your evenings. You can visit both from a single base.

Can you do this as a weekend trip from Vancouver?

Yes, but the ferry and drive take up much of Friday and Sunday. An extra night gives you more time on the coast.

Which town is quieter for a weekend stay?

Choose Ucluelet for a quiet harbor or bluff-side stay and easy access to the Lighthouse Loop.

What should you book first for a weekend getaway?

Lock in lodging and your ferry if you’re coming from the mainland. Then book any lessons or tours.

What can you do if the whole weekend is wet?

Use breaks in the weather for short walks and leave time for coffee, a long lunch, and the aquarium if open.
