Three Days in Tofino and Ucluelet

Beaches, Big Trees, and the Wild Pacific Trail

Length
3 days
Towns
Tofino, Ucluelet
Best for
First-timers and anyone

Three days is a comfortable amount of time to visit both Tofino and Ucluelet. You can take a long beach walk, spend some time in the rainforest, and still get to know the quieter side of the coast. The towns are close enough to visit from one base, which works well for first-timers and anyone who likes to leave room for a surf lesson or boat trip. Keep at least one day loose so you can work around the weather.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Tofino and Chesterman Beach

    Start downtown for coffee and a walk by the docks. Spend the afternoon at Chesterman Beach or book a surf lesson with a local school. Have dinner in Tofino while you’re already at the north end of the route.

  2. Day 2: Pacific Rim National Park Reserve

    Start at Long Beach and take a picnic lunch. In the afternoon, visit an open Rainforest Trail loop. If you have time and the access trail is open, add a walk at Wickaninnish Beach. On a stormy day, choose safe viewpoints and keep well away from the surf.

  3. Day 3: Ucluelet and the Wild Pacific Trail

    Walk the Lighthouse Loop first. Have lunch by the harbor, then choose the aquarium if open or an out-and-back walk from Brown’s Beach. If this is also a ferry day, keep the afternoon stop optional.

Recommended stops

  • Chesterman BeachStart here for a walk or make a lesson your main Tofino outing.
  • Long Beach and Rainforest TrailPair one stretch of sand with one open forest loop.
  • Wild Pacific TrailChoose the Lighthouse Loop and add Brown’s Beach if time allows.

Tips for this trip

  • Put boat outings on the best forecast day and leave room to move the beach plans.
  • Check park admission, surf, and tides. Skip exposed beaches when winds and waves are high.

Frequently asked questions

Where should you stay for three days?

Either town works. Stay in Tofino for a beach or surf trip, or in Ucluelet for the lighthouse and an easier start toward Highway 4 on the way out.

What should you book ahead?

Book lodging and any boat tours or surf lessons first. Check park passes and ferry space as you firm up your dates.

Should you split your stay between Tofino and Ucluelet?

One base works well for three days. Pick where you’d like to spend your evenings.

What should you do in Pacific Rim with just half a day?

Walk a short stretch of Long Beach and one open Rainforest Trail loop if conditions allow.

Can you do this trip in winter?

Yes if you build it around conditions and open sites. Consider a room with an ocean view and fewer fixed outdoor plans.
