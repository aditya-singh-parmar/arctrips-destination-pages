10-Day British Columbia Road Trip

Mountains, Lakes, and the Fraser Canyon

Length
10 days
Towns
Vancouver, Whistler, Okanagan, Kelowna, Penticton
Best for
First-time visitors

Ten days is a good length for a mainland road trip if you enjoy the time between stops. This loop takes in the coast, the mountains, and the drier country around the Okanagan lakes. It suits first-time visitors who want some variety and can give the longer stretches a day of their own. The night in Hope makes the trip back to Vancouver easier. Check road conditions before choosing your route across the mountains, and give yourself room to adjust.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vancouver

    Walk the Stanley Park Seawall and settle in for dinner in the West End.

  2. Day 2: False Creek

    Visit Granville Island and follow the waterfront to Olympic Village or take a small ferry.

  3. Day 3: Sea-to-Sky Highway

    Stop at Shannon Falls and choose lunch or a gondola ride in Squamish. Stay in Whistler.

  4. Day 4: Whistler

    Spend the day on the mountain if lifts are open, or explore the Valley Trail and the Village.

  5. Day 5: Drive to the Okanagan

    Pick the route with the best conditions for your vehicle. If using Highway 99 east via Lillooet, fuel before the remote stretch. Sleep in Kelowna.

  6. Day 6: Kelowna

    Walk or bike Myra Canyon if conditions allow and spend the evening by Okanagan Lake.

  7. Day 7: Penticton

    Stop for coffee in Peachland. Walk at Okanagan Beach and sleep in Penticton.

  8. Day 8: Naramata or Skaha Lake

    Pick a winery with a sober ride, a rail trail walk, or a beach afternoon.

  9. Day 9: Toward Hope

    Drive west via Highway 3 if conditions are good. Stop for lunch in Princeton, then stay in Hope.

  10. Day 10: Fraser Canyon or Vancouver

    With a late finish, make a short out-and-back into the Fraser Canyon, then drive to Vancouver. If you have a flight, go straight to the airport with time to return the car.

Recommended stops

  • Shannon FallsBreak up the northbound drive here.
  • Myra CanyonWalk or bike the trestles on your full Kelowna day.
  • Fraser CanyonMake this a short side trip from Hope if your last day allows.

Tips for this trip

  • Download your driving route and note fuel stops before leaving Whistler.
  • Book a last night near Vancouver if your flight is early.

Frequently asked questions

Is 10 days enough for a BC road trip?

Yes for this part of the mainland. The route has four main bases before the last night in Hope.

What season works best?

Late spring through early fall gives you more daylight. Check road reports and fire or smoke updates for your dates.

Does this road trip include Vancouver Island?

This loop stays on the mainland and uses the extra days for Whistler, the Okanagan, and Hope.

Can you reverse the loop?

Yes. Start with Hope and the Okanagan if the forecast looks clearer there, and finish with Whistler and Vancouver.

How much time should you allow for the drive from Whistler to the Okanagan?

Keep most of the day free. The route, road conditions, and breaks all affect how long it takes.
