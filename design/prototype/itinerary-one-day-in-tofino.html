One Day in Tofino

Tofino’s Beaches and Rainforest

Length
1 day
Towns
Tofino
Best for
Anyone who’s already on the coast and wants an easy day outdoors

Tofino is a small town, but the beaches are a big part of the visit. If you have one day, give yourself time for a proper walk on the sand and a chance to see the rainforest nearby. This suits anyone who’s already on the coast and wants an easy day outdoors. Bring a warm layer even if it feels mild in town, and check the tide and surf before choosing a beach.

The plan

  1. Morning

    Start with coffee downtown, then walk down to the waterfront to see the boats in Clayoquot Sound. Drive out to Chesterman Beach for a walk along the sand. Dress for wind even if it feels mild in town.

  2. Afternoon

    Have lunch near the beaches or in town. Head south to Pacific Rim National Park Reserve for Long Beach and, if trail conditions allow, one loop of the Rainforest Trail. The forest is a good choice on a wet day, as long as there are no wind closures.

  3. Evening

    Return to Tofino for dinner. On a clear day, you can fit in a last walk at Chesterman Beach before dark. During rough surf, watch from a safe viewpoint and stay back from the water and driftwood.

Recommended stops

  • Tofino waterfrontGet coffee near town and look out over Clayoquot Sound.
  • Chesterman BeachMake this your long beach walk if surf and tides are suitable.
  • Rainforest TrailChoose one open boardwalk loop for a short forest walk.

Tips for this trip

  • Check Parks Canada’s trail notices, tides, and swell forecast before you leave. Stay off the beach when the tide or swell is high.
  • Keep a rain shell and dry shoes in the car. A wet beach walk feels better when you can change before dinner.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit Tofino in a day?

Yes, if you’re already on the west coast or nearby. A Vancouver round trip is best spread over several days.

Do you need a pass for Long Beach?

Plan to get a Parks Canada entry pass for your visit to the Long Beach Unit. Check current admission and trail conditions before you go.

Which beaches should you see on your first visit?

Chesterman is close to Tofino. Long Beach gives you a second stretch of coast in the national park reserve.

Can you fit a surf lesson into one day?

Yes. Make it your morning outing and choose either Long Beach or the rainforest for the afternoon.

What should you do in Tofino during a storm?

Watch from an open, designated viewing area or from indoors and follow local closure notices.
