Vancouver, Tofino, and Ucluelet in Five Days

City Seawall to Pacific Surf

Length
5 days
Towns
Vancouver, Tofino, Ucluelet, Nanaimo
Best for
A first visit

Five days gives you a little city time in Vancouver and a real break by the surf in Tofino and Ucluelet. This works well for a first visit if you’re happy to spend part of the trip on the ferry and in the car. Stay in one place on the coast so you get two full days for the beaches and trails. Treat the ferry days as travel days, and leave a good gap before any flight home.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vancouver waterfront

    Walk part of the Stanley Park Seawall and eat lunch at Granville Island. Pick up anything you need for the road trip before dinner.

  2. Day 2: Ferry to Nanaimo and Highway 4

    Take Horseshoe Bay to Departure Bay or Tsawwassen to Duke Point. Drive through Port Alberni and make this a travel day. If you arrive early, stop by the harbor near your hotel.

  3. Day 3: Tofino and the beaches

    Spend the morning at Chesterman Beach or book a surf lesson. Have lunch in town and walk a section of Long Beach on the way back.

  4. Day 4: Ucluelet and the rainforest

    Give the Lighthouse Loop the morning. Have lunch by the harbor and visit an open Rainforest Trail loop after lunch. On a rough-weather day, cut the beach stops and favor safe viewpoints.

  5. Day 5: West coast to Vancouver

    Drive to Nanaimo for your booked ferry. Leave a buffer for the winding road and terminal check-in. If you’re flying, leave the flight for the next day where possible.

Recommended stops

  • Stanley ParkWalk a section of the Seawall on your city day.
  • Chesterman BeachMake this your main beach walk or surf stop.
  • Wild Pacific TrailSave the Lighthouse Loop for your Ucluelet morning.

Tips for this trip

  • Stay in one place for all three west coast nights.
  • Book both ferry directions before adding timed outings around the crossings.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do this trip without a car?

You can piece it together with booked transfers and local rides. This particular plan assumes a car from the mainland or Nanaimo.

What if you have an early flight home?

Sleep on the mainland the night before. A ferry crossing and a long drive leave little room for delays.

Which stop should you cut if a ferry is late?

Skip Cathedral Grove or any other stop you hoped to add on the transfer. Go straight to your west coast lodging.

Where can you stop for supplies before the coast?

Pick up groceries and fuel in Port Alberni on the way west.

Can you fit in a boat outing?

Yes. Use one full-day morning and save a beach or forest walk as the backup.
