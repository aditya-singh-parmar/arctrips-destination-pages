Tofino, British Columbia

Best time to visit Tofino: crowds, whales, waves and storms

Tofino works all year, but the crowds do not. Summer is the busiest and most expensive season, spring and fall are quieter, and winter is the quietest, with storm watching as the reason to come.

On the calendar

The yearly events that shape when people come. Check each organiser for this year's dates.

  • March

    Pacific Rim Whale Festival

    Whale spotting, a parade, food and talks in Tofino, Ucluelet and the park. 2026: March 14 to 21. 2027: March 13 to 20.

  • March

    Herring spawn

    Herring turn the coastal water a milky turquoise and draw eagles, sea lions and even wolves along the shoreline.

  • Late April and early May

    Tofino Shorebird Festival

    Thousands of shorebirds stop at the Tofino mudflats on their northern migration, with guided birding by kayak.

  • May

    Rip Curl Nationals

    Canada's top shortboard surfers compete at Cox Bay for national titles and Team Canada qualification spots.

  • Saturdays, May to October

    Tofino Market

    Local makers and food vendors at the Village Green. Arrive before noon, as popular items sell out.

  • Late September or October

    Queen of the Peak

    An all-women's surf championship celebrating women's and girls' shortboarding and longboarding.

  • October

    Surf Month

    October is officially Surf Month in Tofino, with major local surf events and reliable early winter swells.

  • Mid-October

    Edge to Edge Marathon

    A scenic race along the Wild Pacific Trail and rugged coastline between Tofino and Ucluelet.

  • Late November to early January

    Tofino Hot Cocoa Trail

    A self-guided tour of local cafes and restaurants serving creative takes on hot chocolate.

  • Winter, weather permitting

    Vancouver Island Winter Surf Games

    A rugged winter surf contest that depends on the weather and shows off cold-water dedication.

Dress for it, then go

  • There is no bad weather here, only poor clothing. Pack a proper waterproof jacket in any month.
  • Dress in layers: a fleece under the shell. It feels colder on the water, so add more for boat tours.
  • Wear waterproof shoes or boots on beaches and trails, and water shoes at Hot Springs Cove, where rocks are slippery.
  • In cooler months add a toque and gloves. Surfers need a 4/3mm or 5/4mm wetsuit, plus booties in winter.

Questions about timing a trip

When is Tofino busiest?

Summer, roughly June through early September, is the busiest and most expensive time. July and August are the peak, and popular resorts can need booking 6 to 12 months ahead.

When is Tofino quietest?

Winter, from November to February, is the quietest season and the best value, though holidays and some weekends can still fill. Spring and fall sit in between, with fewer crowds than summer.

When is the best time to see whales near Tofino?

Gray whales pass from March to May, and humpbacks are common from summer into fall. The main whale watching season runs March through November, and most tours do not run in winter.

When is storm watching season in Tofino?

The main season runs from November to March, with the most dramatic and frequent storms from November to February. Stay at least two nights for a better chance of seeing a big one.

When is the best time to surf in Tofino?

Surfing is year-round. Summer suits beginners, with smaller waves but crowded lineups. Spring suits intermediates, and fall and winter bring bigger swells for experienced surfers. October is Surf Month.

What is the weather like?

Cool and ocean-influenced, with mild temperatures all year and wetter months in fall and winter. Dress for it and every season has something to offer.

Stay in Tofino

Places to stay are open in every month. See Ucluelet too, forty minutes down the same road.
