Ucluelet, Vancouver Island

Restaurants in Ucluelet

Fifteen places to eat, grouped by the meal you are actually deciding about. One fine dining room, three morning stops, and a lot of good food after a day outside.

Ucluelet eats early and it eats casual. The exception is Pluvio, which is the reason some people drive out here at all. Everything else is a working harbour town feeding people who have been outside since seven.

Hours move with the season on this coast, so check before you set out. The order below runs from the meal you book weeks ahead to the food you pick up on the way to a trail.

A dinner worth booking

Two rooms for the night you planned the trip around. Both want a reservation.

  1. 01

    Pluvio Restaurant + Rooms

    Pluvio is one of the best-known restaurants in Ucluelet. It is a fine dining restaurant and small boutique inn on Peninsula Road.

    This is the place to choose for a special dinner, anniversary meal, date night, or food-focused trip. The restaurant celebrates local products, seasonal ingredients, and the natural setting of Ucluelet and Vancouver Island.

    The menu is creative and refined, with a strong sense of place. Expect a slower, more thoughtful dining experience rather than a quick casual meal.

    Good forFine dining · Special occasions · Seasonal tasting menus · Local and wild ingredients · Date night · Food lovers

    Good to know. Reservations are strongly recommended. This is one of the most sought-after dining experiences in Ucluelet.

  2. 02

    Currents Restaurant

    Currents Restaurant is another dining option connected with Black Rock Oceanfront Resort. It is a good choice for travelers looking for a more polished meal with west coast views and a quieter atmosphere.

    Good forResort dining · Ocean views · Dinner · Couples · Special meals · West coast atmosphere

    Good to know. Check current hours and seasonal openings before planning your meal.

By the water

Three places where the view is half of what you came for: the small craft harbour, the oceanfront, and a brewery in a former church.

  1. 03

    Floathouse Patio & Grill

    Floathouse Patio & Grill is a popular harbourfront restaurant in Ucluelet. It sits near the small craft harbour and is a good choice if you want seafood, casual dining, and water views.

    The setting is a big part of the experience. You can eat while looking out at boats, harbour activity, and the coastal scenery.

    Good forHarbour views · Seafood · Casual dinner · Lunch · Families · Drinks by the water

    Good to know. This is a good pick when you want a relaxed meal with a strong Ucluelet harbour feel.

  2. 04

    Big Beach Bar & Lounge

    Big Beach Bar & Lounge is a good choice if you want drinks, a relaxed meal, and a coastal resort setting. It is connected with the Black Rock Oceanfront Resort area, close to Big Beach and the Wild Pacific Trail.

    This is a good stop after a walk, especially if you want oceanfront atmosphere without leaving town.

    Good forDrinks · Casual dinner · Lounge food · Oceanfront atmosphere · Couples · After-hike meals

    Good to know. Pair it with a walk near Big Beach or the Wild Pacific Trail.

  3. 05

    Ucluelet Brewing Company

    Ucluelet Brewing Company is a fun stop for craft beer, casual food, and harbour views. It is located in a former church, which gives it a unique local feel.

    This is a good place to relax after hiking the Wild Pacific Trail, kayaking, fishing, or beach exploring.

    Good forCraft beer · Casual meals · Snacks · Harbour views · Groups · Relaxed evenings

    Good to know. It has a lively but relaxed feel and is a good choice for people who want drinks with a local touch.

Breakfast, coffee and baking

Three morning stops, and the ones to hit before a trail, a paddle or the drive through the park.

  1. 06

    Heartwood Kitchen

    Heartwood Kitchen is a cozy Ucluelet favorite for breakfast, brunch, and comfort food made with care. It is a good choice if you want a relaxed meal in town before hiking, kayaking, or exploring the coast.

    The menu focuses on scratch-made food and local ingredients. It is especially good for people who want a warm, welcoming meal rather than something too formal.

    Good forBreakfast · Brunch · Coffee · Casual lunch · Comfort food · Families · Rainy mornings

    Good to know. This is a popular local spot, so it can get busy during peak travel season.

  2. 07

    Zoe’s Bakery & Cafe

    Zoe’s Bakery & Cafe is a popular stop for baked goods, breakfast, lunch, coffee, and sweet treats. It is a good choice before a hike, kayak tour, or drive through Pacific Rim National Park.

    Good forCoffee · Baked goods · Breakfast · Lunch · Pastries · Grab-and-go food

    Good to know. Good for a casual morning stop or a simple lunch.

  3. 08

    Barkley Cafe

    Barkley Cafe is a good local-style cafe stop for coffee, breakfast, lunch, and a relaxed small-town feel. It is useful for travelers who want a casual meal before exploring Ucluelet.

    Good forCoffee · Breakfast · Lunch · Sandwiches · Casual cafe meals · Easy town stop

    Good to know. A good option when you want something simple and comfortable.

Easy and casual

Six for the end of a long day outside, when nobody wants to dress for dinner.

  1. 09

    Cedar Grill

    Cedar Grill is a casual restaurant in Ucluelet with a menu that works for many types of travelers. It is a practical choice for families, groups, and visitors who want a reliable sit-down meal.

    It is often a good option when you want something easy, filling, and central.

    Good forCasual dinner · Families · Groups · Comfort food · Pizza and pub-style meals · Easy town dining

    Good to know. This is a useful choice when you need a flexible menu that works for different tastes.

  2. 10

    Abbondanza Wood Fired Pizza

    Abbondanza is a good choice when you want pizza in Ucluelet. It serves wood-fired pizza and works well for families, groups, and casual nights.

    Pizza is also a useful option if you want something easy after a long outdoor day.

    Good forWood-fired pizza · Casual dinner · Families · Groups · Takeout-style meals · Easy comfort food

    Good to know. Popular casual spots can sell out or have changing hours, so check before going.

  3. 11

    Papi’s Cantina

    Papi’s Cantina is a Mexican restaurant and cocktail bar in Ucluelet. It is a good option if you want tacos, Mexican flavors, drinks, and a casual night out.

    It brings variety to the Ucluelet food scene, especially if you want something other than seafood or pub food.

    Good forMexican food · Tacos · Cocktails · Casual dinner · Groups · A fun evening meal

    Good to know. A good choice when you want bold flavors and a relaxed restaurant setting.

  4. 12

    Ukee Poke

    Ukee Poke is a casual, healthy choice near the Tofino-Ucluelet junction area. It is good for a quick lunch, light dinner, or takeout before a beach or park stop.

    Poke bowls are a nice fit for west coast travel because they feel fresh, customizable, and easy to eat on the go.

    Good forPoke bowls · Healthy lunch · Takeout · Casual dinner · Quick meals · Fresh flavors

    Good to know. This is a good option when you want something lighter than fish and chips or pizza.

  5. 13

    Ukee Dogs Eatery

    Ukee Dogs Eatery is a casual local stop known for hot dogs, tacos, burritos, and coffee. It is simple, relaxed, and useful when you want a quick bite.

    This is not fine dining, but it can be exactly what you need after a walk or before heading out for the day.

    Good forQuick meals · Hot dogs · Tacos · Burritos · Casual snacks · Coffee

    Good to know. Great for a relaxed, no-fuss meal.

  6. 14

    Ukee Snack Shack

    Ukee Snack Shack is a family-owned comfort food stop with smash burgers, donairs, fries, onion rings, and other quick meals. It also offers many gluten-free options.

    This is a good choice for travelers who want something casual, filling, and easy.

    Good forSmash burgers · Donairs · Fries · Quick meals · Gluten-free options · Family-friendly comfort food

    Good to know. A useful pick when you want simple comfort food rather than seafood.

Food to take with you

One stop for the beach day, the trailhead and the road.

  1. 15

    Blackberry Cove Marketplace

    Blackberry Cove Marketplace is useful if you want deli items, groceries, snacks, or picnic food. It is not just a restaurant stop, but it is worth including because many visitors need easy food for hikes, beach days, and road trips.

    Good forPicnic supplies · Snacks · Deli items · Groceries · Road trip food · Easy takeout-style options

    Good to know. Stop here before heading to the Wild Pacific Trail, beaches, or Pacific Rim National Park.

Questions

What people ask about eating in Ucluelet

Four answers from the Ucluelet restaurants guide.

What is the best restaurant in Ucluelet?

Pluvio is often considered one of the top restaurants in Ucluelet for fine dining and special occasions. For casual meals, Jiggers, Heartwood Kitchen, Floathouse Patio & Grill, and Ucluelet Brewing Company are popular choices.

Where should I eat seafood in Ucluelet?

Try Jiggers for casual fish and chips, Floathouse Patio & Grill for harbourfront seafood, Pluvio for refined local seafood, and Ukee Poke for a fresh casual seafood bowl.

What is the best place for breakfast in Ucluelet?

Heartwood Kitchen is a strong choice for breakfast and brunch. Zoe’s Bakery & Cafe and Barkley Cafe are also good morning stops.

Where can I get coffee in Ucluelet?

Try Zoe’s Bakery & Cafe, Barkley Cafe, Heartwood Kitchen, or other local cafe stops in town.
