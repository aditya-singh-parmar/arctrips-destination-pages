Suggested itinerary

Four days on the west coast

Length
4 days
Towns
Tofino, Ucluelet

Tofino's beaches, a day on Clayoquot Sound, then Ucluelet's coast, bog boardwalk, harbor and a paddle.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Tofino beaches

    • Long Beach Part of the Long Beach Unit of Pacific Rim National Park Reserve, between Tofino and Ucluelet. A Parks Canada pass is required.
    • Chesterman Beach A 2.7-kilometer stretch of white sand shaped like a W. Walk it, or take a beginner surf lesson on the sandy bottom.
    • Tonquin Beach A sunset-facing cove reached by an 800-meter gravel path with viewing platforms. Calmer water than the surf beaches.

  2. Day 2: Clayoquot Sound by boat

    • Hot Springs Cove Reached only by water or air from Tofino: a 2-kilometer cedar boardwalk to five natural pools. A typical excursion lasts 6 to 7 hours.
    • Whale and wildlife tour (shorter option) Many tours last about two to three hours. Main whale watching season is March through November, with gray whales in spring.

  3. Day 3: Ucluelet shoreline

    • Wild Pacific Trail, Lighthouse Loop The 2.6-kilometer Lighthouse Loop takes about 45 minutes along black volcanic rock and leads to Amphitrite Point Lighthouse.
    • Amphitrite Point Lighthouse An active beacon on the southern tip of the peninsula, good for spotting migrating whales in spring and sea lions on the offshore rocks.
    • Ucluelet Aquarium A catch-and-release aquarium at the foot of Main Street with touch tanks. Fully indoor and seasonal, typically March through early December.

  4. Day 4: Bog, harbor and paddle

    • Ucluelet Inlet paddle The sheltered harbor suits beginners and a calm morning paddle past fishing docks, with bald eagles and kingfishers along the shore.
    • Shorepine Bog Trail An 800-meter boardwalk loop through a stunted forest in the national park. Stay on the boardwalk to protect the slow-growing plants.
    • Whiskey Dock and Ucluelet Harbour Walk A historic pier with one of the best views of the harbor, next to boardwalks through the working boat basin.

Tips for this trip

  • Every season works. Summer is busiest, spring and fall are quieter, winter is for storm watching.
