7-Day Family Road Trip Around Vancouver Island

Little Walks and Big Island Beaches

Length
7 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Parksville, Ucluelet, Nanaimo

Vancouver Island is a good place for a family road trip if you keep the days loose. There are easy harbor walks, beaches where you can stop for a while, and towns with a place to eat when everyone gets hungry at once. A week gives families with young kids room for naps, short walks, and the odd rainy afternoon indoors. Break up the drive west and stay close to the ferry on the last night if you have an early sailing.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria harbor

    Walk the Inner Harbour and watch the boats. Pick a hotel where you can walk to dinner.

  2. Day 2: Beacon Hill Park or museum

    Follow short park paths and stop when the kids have had enough. On a wet day, visit the Royal BC Museum if open.

  3. Day 3: Cowichan Valley to Parksville

    Stop for lunch in Cowichan Bay or at Transfer Beach in Ladysmith. Stay in Parksville and keep the evening easy.

  4. Day 4: Parksville to Ucluelet

    Give the kids a short walk at Rathtrevor Beach if conditions are good. Stop in Port Alberni for lunch and a bathroom break. Check in for two nights on the west coast.

  5. Day 5: Lighthouse and beach

    Try Ucluelet’s Lighthouse Loop first. In the afternoon, choose the aquarium if open or a short walk on Long Beach in safe conditions. Keep children away from surf, wet rocks, and driftwood during rough conditions.

  6. Day 6: Head back to Nanaimo

    Make one easy stop on the drive east. Check in near Nanaimo and walk the harbor before dinner if everyone has energy.

  7. Day 7: Ferry day

    Get breakfast and go to the right Nanaimo terminal for your reservation. If you have a late sailing, let the kids choose one last short stop.

Recommended stops

  • Beacon Hill ParkChoose a short loop you can cut back if needed.
  • Rathtrevor BeachPlan a walk if weather and tides suit your group.
  • Ucluelet Aquarium or Lighthouse LoopPick the aquarium when open or a short trail outing in safe conditions.

Tips for this trip

  • Choose rooms with a kitchen or fridge so breakfast and road snacks are easy.
  • Keep the ferry confirmation handy and leave room for bathroom and snack breaks on Highway 4.

Frequently asked questions

Which stops work with young children?

The Inner Harbour, Nanaimo’s waterfront, and a short section of the Lighthouse Loop are easy to cut short. Check path surfaces if using a stroller.

Where should families stay?

A place with a kitchen near Parksville or Ucluelet makes early meals and snack packing easier. Downtown Victoria helps cut driving on the first two days.

How can you break up the drive to Ucluelet with kids?

Plan your main stop in Port Alberni for lunch and a bathroom break. Add another only if everyone feels up to it.

Can you do this trip with a stroller?

Use the harbor walks for smoother outings and check trail surfaces before taking a stroller to beaches or bluffs.

What are good rainy-day stops for kids?

Try the Royal BC Museum in Victoria or the Ucluelet Aquarium when open.
