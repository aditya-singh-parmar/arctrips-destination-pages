Two days at the Pacific Rim Whale Festival

Length
2 days
Towns
Tofino, Ucluelet

The festival guide's own one-day plan followed by its weekend list: whale spotting, a tour, food, a talk and a coastal hike.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Festival day plan

    • Morning whale spotting or beach walk Learn to look for blows, dark backs and flukes. Bring binoculars, warm layers and patience, as sightings are never guaranteed.
    • Midday festival event or market Look for the Parade of Whales and Wonders, which opens the week, or the Chowder Chowdown, where local restaurants and chefs showcase coastal chowder.
    • Afternoon Wild Pacific Trail or Long Beach Coastal walks in Ucluelet or the national park. Dress for cool, wet, windy March conditions.
    • Dinner in Ucluelet or Tofino Support local restaurants. Choose a mix of outdoor and indoor events in case the weather changes.

  2. Day 2: Festival weekend list

    • Whale watching tour Spring tours focus on the gray whale migration, from Barkley Sound or Clayoquot Sound. Reserve ahead, as tours and lodging are in demand.
    • Food or art event The festival mixes free and ticketed events, including food, art, markets and family activities. Check ticket details first.
    • Speaker or education event Talks, workshops and education sessions on whales, marine mammals and coastal ecosystems. Some are free, others need registration.
    • Coastal hike Walk the Wild Pacific Trail or take a rainforest walk, and leave time to drive between the towns and the national park.

Tips for this trip

  • March. 2026 festival dates are March 14 to 21, and 2027 dates are March 13 to 20.
