Seven Days on Vancouver Island

Island Harbors, Surf, and Waterfalls

Length
7 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Ucluelet, Tofino, Campbell River, Nanaimo

A week is enough to see how different Vancouver Island feels from one side to the other. There’s city life around Victoria, open surf on the west coast, and quieter water near Campbell River. This is a good first road trip for travelers who want a bit of each and are fine with a couple of longer drives. Staying more than one night in the coastal areas makes the drive worthwhile. Check Highway 4 and leave time to reach your final ferry.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria

    Walk the Inner Harbour and Chinatown, then head to Beacon Hill Park. Stay downtown.

  2. Day 2: Gardens and Cowichan Bay

    Visit Butchart Gardens, then cross the Malahat for a late lunch or dinner in Cowichan Bay. Sleep near Duncan or Parksville, depending on how much driving you want.

  3. Day 3: Highway 4 to Ucluelet

    Stop in Port Alberni for fuel and food. Walk the Lighthouse Loop if you reach Ucluelet in daylight. Stay on the west coast.

  4. Day 4: Pacific Rim and Tofino

    Walk Long Beach and one open Rainforest Trail loop. Have dinner in Tofino or back near your room.

  5. Day 5: Cross to Campbell River

    Drive back through Port Alberni, then turn north on the east side. Stop for lunch near Parksville or Qualicum Beach. Stay in Campbell River.

  6. Day 6: Elk Falls and the waterfront

    Visit Elk Falls Provincial Park if trails are open. Leave the afternoon for the waterfront or book a guided wildlife outing that fits the season.

  7. Day 7: Nanaimo and the ferry

    Drive south, pick up lunch near Nanaimo’s harbor if time allows, and check in at the terminal on your booking.

Recommended stops

  • Butchart GardensStart here before you cross the Malahat.
  • Pacific RimPair a Long Beach walk with one open Rainforest Trail loop.
  • Elk FallsKeep a morning free for the park when trails are open.

Tips for this trip

  • Plan meals and fuel around Port Alberni both ways across Highway 4.
  • Stay in Campbell River on both nights so the wildlife or waterfall day needs little driving.

Frequently asked questions

Is seven days enough for Vancouver Island?

Yes for Victoria, Pacific Rim, and one central Island base. Save the far north for another trip.

Do you need to book the ferry early?

Reserve vehicle space once dates are set, especially for weekends and holidays. Check the terminal and check-in window.

Which way should you go first on Vancouver Island?

Start in Victoria if you’re coming through Swartz Bay and finish near Nanaimo for the ferry back.

Can you stay on the west coast longer?

Yes. Trade the Campbell River section for two more coastal nights if beaches and boat tours are your main draw.

What should you do if you miss the lighthouse on Day 3?

Walk the loop early on Day 4 or save it for the morning you leave and shorten another stop.
