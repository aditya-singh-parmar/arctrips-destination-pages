Three days from Squamish to Whistler

Length
3 days
Towns
Squamish, Whistler

Squamish views, a Squamish adventure day, then a Whistler resort day with gondola, lake, shopping and spa.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Squamish Nature and Views

    • Shannon Falls A tall waterfall dropping down granite cliffs after a short, easy walk from the parking area, beside Stawamus Chief Provincial Park.
    • Sea to Sky Gondola Rides up for views over Howe Sound, Shannon Falls and the Stawamus Chief, with easy trails, lookout platforms and a summit lodge at the top.
    • Easy hike or viewing platforms Walk easy trails at the top of the gondola and enjoy the lookout platforms over Howe Sound.
    • Dinner in Squamish A growing local food and craft beer scene.

  2. Day 2: Squamish Adventure

    • Hike the Chief, climb, mountain bike or try a guided activity Squamish's most famous landmark. The hike is steep and challenging, with stairs, rocks, roots and three peaks; the First Peak is the shortest summit.
    • Brewery or cafe Slow down between bigger outdoor activities with coffee or a local brewery.
    • Drive to Whistler in the evening Whistler is farther north along the Sea to Sky Highway than Squamish.

  3. Day 3: Whistler Resort Day

    • Peak 2 Peak Gondola Connects Whistler and Blackcomb mountains with big alpine views. Mountain temperatures are often 5 to 10°C cooler than the village, so dress in layers.
    • Alpine walk or Lost Lake Close to the village but peaceful and forested. Swim, picnic, walk, bike or paddle in summer; snowshoe or cross-country ski in winter.
    • Village shopping Wander between shops, galleries, gear stores and cafes in the walkable village.
    • Spa, dinner or nightlife Outdoor hot baths, cold plunges, steam rooms and saunas in a forest setting. Quiet and phone-free.

Tips for this trip

  • Both are great in summer. Whistler is best in winter for snow; Squamish is a quieter winter stop.
