Vancouver Island

Eleven towns, the west coast first, then south to north.

Vancouver Island overview

The west coast, at the end of Highway 4

One road leaves the east coast near Parksville and crosses the mountains to the open Pacific. It ends in these two, forty minutes apart.

  1. 01 Ucluelet The Wild Pacific Trail and its Lighthouse Loop, Big Beach, the islands of Barkley Sound, and the gray whales in spring.
  2. 02 Tofino Long Beach, Chesterman Beach, Cox Bay, the Tonquin Trail and the islands of Clayoquot Sound.

The south end

Where the ferries and the flights land, and the only part of the island with a city on it.

  1. 03 Victoria The BC Parliament Buildings, the Inner Harbour, Beacon Hill Park, Fisherman's Wharf and the Royal BC Museum.
  2. 04 Sooke Sooke Potholes, East Sooke Regional Park, Whiffin Spit, Sheringham Point Lighthouse and the start of the Juan de Fuca Marine Trail.
  3. 05 Sidney Sidney Spit in the Gulf Islands National Park Reserve, The Butchart Gardens, and whale watching off the Saanich Peninsula.
  4. 06 Shawnigan Lake The Kinsol Trestle, the lake itself, the Cowichan Valley Trail and the climb up Old Baldy Mountain.

Up the east coast

Sheltered water, warm shallow beaches and the crossings back to the mainland.

  1. 07 Chemainus The murals, the Chemainus Theatre Festival, Waterwheel Park and the harbor views.
  2. 08 Nanaimo Saysutshun, the Harbourfront Walkway, Pipers Lagoon, Ammonite Falls and the crossing to Gabriola Island.
  3. 09 Nanoose Moorecroft Regional Park, the Notch Hill loop, Beachcomber Regional Park and the tide pools along the bay.
  4. 10 Parksville Rathtrevor Beach, the two kilometer waterfront walkway, Cathedral Grove and Englishman River Falls.

North

Where the island narrows, the current runs hard through Discovery Passage and the salmon come through.

  1. 11 Campbell River Elk Falls, the Rotary Seawalk, Discovery Fishing Pier, the Ripple Rock Trail and the way in to Strathcona Provincial Park.

The Mainland

Vancouver, the Sea to Sky towns, and the Kootenays.

Mainland overview

Vancouver and the Sea to Sky

The city, then one road north past Howe Sound to the granite of Squamish and the mountains of Whistler.

  1. 01 Vancouver Stanley Park, Granville Island, the Seawall, Gastown, and a mix of cuisines, arts and traditions from around the world.
  2. 02 Squamish The Stawamus Chief, the Sea to Sky Gondola, Shannon Falls, mountain biking, rock climbing and wind sports.
  3. 03 Whistler Whistler Blackcomb, skiing and snowboarding, the Peak 2 Peak Gondola, mountain biking, alpine hiking and lakes.

The Kootenays

Inland, above Kootenay Lake, with the Selkirk Mountains behind.

  1. 04 Nelson Steep old-school singletrack, Gerrard rainbow trout on Kootenay Lake, and a strong local trail culture.
