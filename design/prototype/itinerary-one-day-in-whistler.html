One Day in Whistler

Whistler Village to the Peaks

Length
1 day
Towns
Whistler
Best for
First-time visitors

Whistler is a mountain town with plenty to do down in the Village, too. With one day, choose one thing you really want to do, like riding the gondola, and leave time to look around. This works well for first-time visitors, especially if you’re coming from Vancouver and have to drive back that evening. Check the lift hours before making plans. If you’re here to ski, it’s easy to spend the whole day on the mountain.

The plan

  1. Morning

    Get coffee near Village Stroll and check the mountain forecast before buying a sightseeing ticket. If lifts are running and the peaks are clear, ride up early. In winter, a ski day can fill the whole schedule on its own.

  2. Afternoon

    Walk to Lost Lake when the route is suitable for walking. In winter, check which trails are open to walkers and which are set aside for skiing. For an indoor afternoon, visit the Squamish Lil’wat Cultural Centre or the Audain Art Museum if open.

  3. Evening

    Eat in Whistler Village. If you’re driving back to Vancouver, give yourself time for the Sea-to-Sky Highway and check road conditions before leaving.

Recommended stops

  • Village StrollStart here for coffee and finish nearby for dinner.
  • Whistler BlackcombChoose a sightseeing ride or a ski day when lifts run.
  • Squamish Lil’wat Cultural CentreMake this your main afternoon stop if clouds cover the peaks.

Tips for this trip

  • Check lift hours and the last ride down before heading up.
  • Leave time for the drive back on Highway 99, especially if you’re adding a stop in Squamish.

Frequently asked questions

Is Whistler worth a day trip from Vancouver?

Yes. Start early and pick either a few highway stops or a longer visit on the mountain.

Can you visit Whistler without skiing?

Yes. Village paths, museums, and sightseeing lifts can fill the day. Check the lift calendar, especially in spring and fall.

Is the PEAK 2 PEAK Gondola open year-round?

Its schedule changes with the season. Check the resort’s hours for your dates.

What can you do for free in Whistler Village?

Walk the pedestrian streets, explore around the Village, or follow an open Valley Trail section.

Can you visit Whistler as a day trip without a car?

Yes. Book a coach from Vancouver and check your last ride back before making dinner plans.
