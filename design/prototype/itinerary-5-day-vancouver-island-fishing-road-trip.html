5-Day Vancouver Island Fishing Road Trip

Two Ports, Two Fishing Days

Length
5 days
Towns
Nanaimo, Ucluelet, Campbell River, Port Alberni
Best for
Anglers

Fishing out of Ucluelet feels different from heading out from Campbell River. In five days, you can try both ports and still have a day between charters to cross the Island. This trip suits anglers who enjoy seeing new water and are happy to spend much of that middle day driving. Choose charter dates with some care. Ask each guide what’s in season, how they handle weather changes, and what you’ll need to keep your catch cold between stops.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Nanaimo to Ucluelet

    Arrive in Nanaimo and cross the Island via Port Alberni. Stay near Ucluelet’s harbor and confirm the charter location before dinner.

  2. Day 2: Fish out of Ucluelet

    Spend the day with a guide. Check the license, salmon stamp if keeping salmon, and the rules for the exact fishing area. Arrange packing or freezing before leaving the dock.

  3. Day 3: Ucluelet to Campbell River

    Pick up processed fish at the agreed time and keep it properly chilled for the drive. Head east on Highway 4, then north on the Island Highway. Sleep in Campbell River.

  4. Day 4: Fish out of Campbell River

    Book a second guided day. Your guide can explain where you’ll fish and what conditions allow. Confirm fish pickup and transport plans before the trip.

  5. Day 5: Campbell River to Nanaimo

    Pick up any fish, then travel south to Nanaimo. Eat by the harbor if you have spare time before your booked sailing.

Recommended stops

  • Ucluelet harborConfirm your first charter and processing plan here.
  • Port AlberniUse this for fuel and lunch during the long transfer.
  • Campbell River waterfrontStay nearby for an easier start on the second charter.

Tips for this trip

  • Confirm each processor’s pickup hours before you set your departure time.
  • Book lodging with space to store your gear and sort out cooler or freezer access ahead of time.

Frequently asked questions

What if the weather cancels one charter?

Ask about rescheduling policies before booking. Keep the travel day open enough for a moved trip, or extend your stay at one port.

Do the same rules apply at both ports?

A BC tidal license covers tidal waters, but openings, catch limits, and restrictions depend on the exact area. Check DFO for each port.

Is there time to visit Tofino on this fishing trip?

You can fit in a short beach stop if the fishing and pickup schedule allows. Add a night for a full visit.

Which port should you fish first?

Start in Ucluelet on this route. Reverse the order if your guides’ best dates or ferry plans point the other way.

Can a beginner join both charters?

Yes. Tell each guide what experience the group has and ask what equipment and help are included.
