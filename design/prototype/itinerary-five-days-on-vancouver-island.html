Five Days on Vancouver Island

Victoria to the Pacific Rim

Length
5 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Parksville, Ucluelet, Tofino, Nanaimo

Five days is enough to get a taste of Victoria and the west coast of Vancouver Island. Breaking up the drive on the east side gives you time in the smaller towns and keeps the trip out to the surf from feeling like one long day in the car. It’s a good route for a first visit if you like city walks as much as wild beaches. Give Highway 4 some extra time, especially on the day you’re catching a ferry.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Downtown Victoria

    Walk the Inner Harbour, Government Street, and Chinatown. Spend the afternoon at Beacon Hill Park or the Royal BC Museum. Sleep downtown so you can walk to dinner.

  2. Day 2: Cowichan Bay and Parksville

    Cross the Malahat and have lunch in Cowichan Bay. Choose downtown Duncan or a short stop in Ladysmith on the way north. Spend the night near Parksville.

  3. Day 3: Cathedral Grove to Ucluelet

    Check MacMillan Park advisories before a Cathedral Grove stop. If the parking area is full, keep going and take a break in Port Alberni. Reach Ucluelet in time for the Lighthouse Loop before dark. Sleep on the west coast.

  4. Day 4: Pacific Rim and Tofino

    Walk at Long Beach early, then choose a Rainforest Trail loop or a stop at Chesterman Beach. Have lunch in Tofino and stay on the west coast again.

  5. Day 5: Return to Nanaimo

    Leave the coast after breakfast. Stop in Port Alberni for fuel, then go straight to your Nanaimo terminal if you have a reservation. Take a waterfront walk only if you have a comfortable buffer.

Recommended stops

  • Cowichan BayStop here for lunch before driving north.
  • Cathedral GroveWalk among the tall trees if parking and trails are open.
  • Lighthouse Loop and Long BeachUse separate days for these two main coastal stops.

Tips for this trip

  • Keep your ferry day free of long walks or tours.
  • Buy food and fuel in Port Alberni before the last leg west.

Frequently asked questions

Where should you stay for five days?

Try one night in Victoria, one around Parksville, and two on the west coast.

Is five days enough to see Vancouver Island?

Yes for the south and Pacific Rim. Give the north Island a longer trip of its own.

What’s the best place for a stop between Parksville and Ucluelet?

Try Cathedral Grove if there’s parking. Port Alberni is a good place for food and fuel.

Can you do this in reverse?

Yes. Start in Nanaimo, spend two nights on the west coast, and finish in Victoria.

Should you visit both Tofino and Ucluelet?

Yes. Use Ucluelet for the Lighthouse Loop and Tofino for a meal and a beach walk.
