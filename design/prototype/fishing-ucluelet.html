Fishing in Ucluelet

Widely regarded as the premier place for saltwater fishing on Vancouver Island, with Big Bank and the offshore reefs a short run from the harbor.

Ucluelet keeps the soul of a working fishing village. Its geography lets boats reach the productive Big Bank and offshore reefs quickly, where the nutrient-rich water of the Pacific shelf supports big runs of salmon and halibut.

A fishing charter here is close to a private wildlife tour as well: humpback whales, porpoises and sea otters are a common sight while trolling the coastal water.

What you fish for

The fish the charters target, and what happens to your catch when you are back at the dock.

  1. 01

    Salmon

    The water off Ucluelet is a major corridor for migrating Chinook (kings, the largest of the salmon) and coho (silvers). Peak season for Chinook usually runs June through August.

    Good forChinook · Coho · Full-day trips · First charters

    Good to know. A salmon conservation stamp goes with your tidal license if you plan to keep salmon.

  2. 02

    Halibut and bottom fishing

    Ucluelet is known for consistent halibut fishing. Charters target these big flatfish on the gravel plateaus offshore, alongside lingcod and several kinds of rockfish.

    Good forHalibut · Lingcod · Rockfish · Full-day trips to the banks

    Good to know. Full-day trips are usually recommended to reach the more consistent offshore banks.

  3. 03

    Wildlife on the water

    While the focus is the catch, it is common to see humpback whales, pods of porpoises and sea otters while trolling the coast.

    Good forWhales · Porpoises · Sea otters

    Good to know. Bring binoculars and a camera in a dry bag.

  4. 04

    Getting your catch home

    After a good day, most charters work with local processors. Your catch can be cleaned, filleted, vacuum-packed and flash-frozen, or smoked, to ship home or take with you.

    Good to know. Confirm cleaning, packing, freezing and pickup with your guide before the trip, and ask about fees.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What can you catch in Ucluelet?

Trips may target salmon, halibut, or other species when conditions and current rules allow. Check the exact area and ask the guide before you go.

When is the best time to fish from Ucluelet?

Chinook salmon peak from June through August, and can also be caught from March to May and in September. October to February is generally quiet. Halibut fishing is consistent.

Do you need a fishing license on a charter?

Yes. Get your own BC tidal waters sport fishing license before boarding. You need the salmon conservation stamp if you keep Pacific salmon. Both are sold online through Fisheries and Oceans Canada.

How long is a fishing charter?

Most charters offer half-day trips of 4 to 6 hours or full-day trips of 8 to 10 hours. Full days are generally recommended to reach the offshore banks.

What should you wear on a fishing charter?

Pack warm layers and a waterproof outer layer, even if the forecast looks mild. Charters supply rods, reels, tackle, bait and safety gear. If you get seasick, take something before you board: the Pacific has a long swell even on calm days.
