14-Day Vancouver Island and Mainland BC Adventure

From Vancouver Island Surf to the Okanagan

Length
14 days
Towns
Vancouver, Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Parksville, Ucluelet, Tofino and 4 more

Two weeks gives you time to see both Vancouver Island and the mainland without being on the road every day. The west coast is worth a few nights on its own, and the mainland part gives you a change of scene with mountains and warmer lake country. This is a good first visit for people who like a mix of city walks, open beaches, and outdoor days. Keep the ferry days simple and check where you can return your rental car before you book flights.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vancouver

    Get settled and walk the waterfront near Coal Harbour.

  2. Day 2: Vancouver

    Spend the morning in Stanley Park and the afternoon at Granville Island.

  3. Day 3: Ferry to Victoria

    Sail from Tsawwassen to Swartz Bay, then explore the Inner Harbour.

  4. Day 4: Victoria

    Visit Chinatown and Beacon Hill Park, or spend half the day at Butchart Gardens.

  5. Day 5: Cowichan Valley to Parksville

    Stop at Cowichan Bay for lunch and sleep near Parksville.

  6. Day 6: Ucluelet

    Drive Highway 4 via Port Alberni. Walk the Lighthouse Loop if you have daylight. Stay three nights on the coast.

  7. Day 7: Tofino

    Walk Chesterman Beach or take a surf lesson. Eat lunch in town.

  8. Day 8: Pacific Rim

    Pick Long Beach and an open Rainforest Trail loop, or use today for a rescheduled boat outing.

  9. Day 9: Nanaimo to the mainland

    Drive back across Highway 4 and sail from Departure Bay to Horseshoe Bay. Stay in North Vancouver or nearby.

  10. Day 10: Sea-to-Sky Highway

    Stop in Squamish for lunch and Shannon Falls. Continue to Whistler.

  11. Day 11: Whistler

    Choose mountain sightseeing, skiing, or a valley walk based on the season.

  12. Day 12: Drive to Kelowna

    Give this transfer a full day. Set your route using DriveBC and check fuel stops before you leave.

  13. Day 13: Myra Canyon and Okanagan Lake

    Visit the trestles if open and spend the afternoon downtown. Pick a lakeside dinner.

  14. Day 14: Kelowna or return west

    Walk the waterfront before a Kelowna departure. If returning your car in Vancouver, use the day for the drive.

Recommended stops

  • Victoria’s Inner HarbourKeep your first Island afternoon near the harbor.
  • Pacific RimGive Tofino, Ucluelet, and the park stops their own time over three nights.
  • Whistler Blackcomb and Myra CanyonSave a full day in each region for these outings.

Tips for this trip

  • Book a night near Horseshoe Bay or North Vancouver after the return ferry if you want an easy start toward Squamish.
  • Keep food and fuel stops in mind for Highway 4 and the drive east from Whistler.

Frequently asked questions

How many ferry rides does this trip need?

Two vehicle crossings. Book the outbound and return terminals separately so each reservation matches the route.

Can you add more of wine country?

Yes. Swap the Vancouver second day for another Okanagan night or extend the trip by one day to reach Penticton and Naramata.

Where should you rent the car?

If you’re staying downtown at first, pick it up before the first ferry. Confirm you can return it in Kelowna if needed.

Can you drive the whole route in a loop?

Yes. Use the last day to drive back to Vancouver, then fly the next day if possible.

Which days should you keep open for weather?

Keep the full Pacific Rim days and your Whistler mountain day loose so you can swap walks, lifts, and tours.
