5-Day Vancouver Island Food and Seafood Tour

A Taste of the Island Coast

Length
5 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Nanaimo, Ucluelet, Tofino
Best for
Travelers

Food is a good reason to take the slow road across Vancouver Island. Victoria has plenty for a whole day of eating and walking, and there are small bakeries, farm shops, and seafood spots farther along the route. Five days suits travelers who like to plan around a few good meals and leave room for what looks good that day. Grouping stops along the drive keeps you close to the next meal. Check opening days, especially midweek or outside summer.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria’s Inner Harbour and Chinatown

    Walk the harbor then head into Chinatown for lunch. Book dinner downtown and ask what seafood is in season.

  2. Day 2: Victoria neighborhoods

    Start with a bakery in James Bay or downtown. Walk Beacon Hill Park before lunch, then choose seafood at Fisherman’s Wharf if vendors are open or a restaurant downtown.

  3. Day 3: Cowichan Bay and the valley

    Try a bakery or a waterfront lunch in Cowichan Bay. Spend the afternoon at a farm shop or cider stop, using a sober driver for tastings. Stay near Duncan.

  4. Day 4: Nanaimo bars to Ucluelet

    Pick up a Nanaimo bar downtown and grab snacks in Port Alberni for the drive west. Save dinner for Ucluelet and book ahead if there’s a place you want to try.

  5. Day 5: Tofino lunch and beach walk

    Start at Chesterman Beach and eat lunch in town. Ask what fish is available that day. If you need an early ferry, stay near your return route and save Tofino for a longer trip.

Recommended stops

  • Chinatown, VictoriaWalk Fisgard Street and leave time for lunch.
  • Cowichan BayUse the waterfront for a bakery or long lunch before visiting a valley farm shop.
  • Nanaimo and UclueletSave room for a Nanaimo bar before a seafood dinner on the west coast.

Tips for this trip

  • Book the most important dinners ahead and ask about that day’s seafood.
  • Carry a cooler if you’re shopping for goods that need to stay cold on the drive.

Frequently asked questions

What foods should you try on Vancouver Island?

Try a Nanaimo bar, island-grown produce in season, and whatever seafood the kitchen recommends that day.

Can you do this without drinking alcohol?

Yes. Choose farm shops, bakeries, coffee, and harbor lunches. The valley day works just as well without tastings.

Where should you eat seafood on Vancouver Island?

Victoria, Ucluelet, and Tofino all work. Ask about the day’s catch and pick a meal near your lodging.

What is a Nanaimo bar?

It’s a layered dessert with a crumb base, a custard-flavored filling, and a chocolate top.

What if you have an early ferry on Day 5?

Skip the Tofino lunch and make Ucluelet dinner your last big meal. Stop for a Nanaimo snack if you still have time.
