Two days on the west coast

Length
2 days
Towns
Tofino, Ucluelet

Beach, rainforest and a sunset cove in Tofino, then the Wild Pacific Trail, lighthouse and harbor in Ucluelet.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Tofino beaches and rainforest

    • Chesterman Beach A 2.7-kilometer stretch of white sand shaped like a W. Walk it, or take a beginner surf lesson on the sandy bottom.
    • Rainforest Trail Two boardwalk loops through the old-growth forest of Pacific Rim National Park Reserve, right off the Pacific Rim Highway.
    • Tonquin Beach A sunset-facing cove reached by an 800-meter gravel path with viewing platforms. Calmer water than the surf beaches.

  2. Day 2: Ucluelet coast

    • Wild Pacific Trail, Lighthouse Loop The 2.6-kilometer Lighthouse Loop takes about 45 minutes along black volcanic rock and leads to Amphitrite Point Lighthouse.
    • Ancient Cedars Loop Passes two red cedars estimated to be over 800 years old. The nearby Rocky Bluffs are a whale watching vantage point in migration season.
    • Ucluelet Aquarium A catch-and-release aquarium at the foot of Main Street with touch tanks. Fully indoor and seasonal, typically March through early December.
    • Ucluelet Harbour Walk Boardwalks through the inner boat basin, with fishing vessels, herons, bald eagles and the occasional sea otter.

Tips for this trip

  • Every season works. Summer is busiest, spring and fall are quieter, winter is for storm watching.
