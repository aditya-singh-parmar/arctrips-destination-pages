Two Days in Victoria

Victoria’s Harbor and Garden Paths

Length
2 days
Towns
Victoria, Sidney

Victoria has plenty to keep you busy downtown, but two days also gives you time to see a little of the Saanich Peninsula. It’s a good length for a first visit, especially if you like both city walks and gardens. Keep the places you can reach on foot together and give the garden visit a morning of its own. If you’re leaving from Swartz Bay that day, check the ferry time before deciding how long to spend on the peninsula.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Inner Harbour, Chinatown, and James Bay

    Walk the Inner Harbour before it gets busy. Head up Government Street toward Chinatown for lunch, then make your way to Beacon Hill Park. If it rains, spend the afternoon at the Royal BC Museum. For dinner, try James Bay or the streets near the harbor.

  2. Day 2: Butchart Gardens and the peninsula

    Start at Butchart Gardens in Brentwood Bay. Give yourself time for the Sunken Garden and a break indoors if it’s cool. For lunch, stay near Brentwood Bay or head to Sidney for a walk along the waterfront. If you’re catching a ferry at Swartz Bay, check your terminal arrival time before adding Sidney.

Recommended stops

  • ChinatownWalk Fisgard Street and take a short detour through Fan Tan Alley.
  • Butchart GardensAllow time for the Sunken Garden and a sit-down break.
  • Sidney waterfrontAdd a walk here if there’s time before your ferry or return to town.

Tips for this trip

  • Book a downtown room so you can walk to dinner after both days.
  • Check garden admission and transit for your dates. Keep Sidney optional on the day you leave.

Frequently asked questions

Is two days enough for Victoria?

Yes. Use one day for the city and one for the gardens. Add a third day if you also want a whale tour or a long day on the coast.

Can you visit Butchart Gardens without a car?

Yes. Look up the current transit connections or book a tour with transportation from Victoria.

How much time should you allow at Butchart Gardens?

Set aside a few hours so you can see the main paths and stop for a break.

Where should you stay for two days in Victoria?

The Inner Harbour and James Bay are both handy for the first day’s walks.

What’s a good rainy-day stop with children?

Try the Royal BC Museum or take a shorter harbor walk followed by lunch indoors.
