Region, British Columbia

Vancouver Island, end to end

Eleven towns on one long island, from Victoria in the south to Campbell River in the north, with the surf coast reached by a single road west through the mountains.

That road ends in Tofino and Ucluelet, 42 km and about 40 minutes apart. They are the two towns taking bookings now, 292 places to stay from $320 a night.

The eleven towns
Where we publish first

Ucluelet and Tofino first, then the island end to end

Ucluelet and Tofino lead, because they are the two towns taking bookings now and the two we have walked properly. Behind them the island runs south to north, from the ferry terminals and the airport at the south end to the salmon water above Campbell River. Those nine open on their photographs and the places worth the drive, with their full guides still being walked.

Rest of British Columbia

The end of Highway 4

One road leaves the east coast near Parksville and crosses the mountains to the open Pacific. It ends in these two, and these two are the ones taking bookings now.

  1. 01 UclueletBook tonight The Wild Pacific Trail and its Lighthouse Loop, Big Beach, the islands of Barkley Sound, and the gray whales in spring.
  2. 02 TofinoBook tonight Long Beach, Chesterman Beach, Cox Bay, the Tonquin Trail and the islands of Clayoquot Sound.

The south end

Where the ferries and the flights land, and the only part of the island with a city on it.

  1. 03 Victoria The Butchart Gardens, the Inner Harbour, Beacon Hill Park, Fisherman's Wharf and the Royal BC Museum.
  2. 04 Sooke Sooke Potholes, East Sooke Regional Park, Whiffin Spit, Sheringham Point Lighthouse and the start of the Juan de Fuca Marine Trail.
  3. 05 Sidney Sidney Spit in the Gulf Islands National Park Reserve, The Butchart Gardens, and whale watching off the Saanich Peninsula.
  4. 06 Shawnigan Lake The Kinsol Trestle, the lake itself, the Cowichan Valley Trail and the climb up Old Baldy Mountain.

Up the east coast

Sheltered water, warm shallow beaches and the crossings back to the mainland.

  1. 07 Chemainus The murals, the Chemainus Theatre Festival, Waterwheel Park and the harbour views.
  2. 08 Nanaimo Saysutshun, the Harbourfront Walkway, Pipers Lagoon, Ammonite Falls and the crossing to Gabriola Island.
  3. 09 Nanoose Moorecroft Regional Park, the Notch Hill loop, Beachcomber Regional Park and the tide pools along the bay.
  4. 10 Parksville Rathtrevor Beach, the two kilometre waterfront walkway, Cathedral Grove and Englishman River Falls.

North

Where the island narrows, the current runs hard through Discovery Passage and the salmon come through.

  1. 11 Campbell River Elk Falls, the Rotary Seawalk, Discovery Fishing Pier, the Ripple Rock Trail and the way in to Strathcona Provincial Park.
