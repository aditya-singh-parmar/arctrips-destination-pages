Vancouver and Whistler in Five Days

Vancouver and Whistler City-to-Summit Escape

Length
5 days
Towns
Vancouver, Whistler

Vancouver and Whistler are close enough to put on one trip, even if you only have five days. They work well together if you like a busy waterfront one day and a view of the mountains the next. This is a good first trip for anyone who wants city time and a full day in Whistler. Give the Sea-to-Sky drive some breathing room if you’re renting a car. A coach is an easy choice if you’re happy to go straight to the Village.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Stanley Park and English Bay

    Follow the Seawall from Coal Harbour or walk a short loop near the totem poles. Eat dinner in the West End and walk down to English Bay if the sky is clear.

  2. Day 2: Granville Island and False Creek

    Buy lunch at the Public Market and take a small ferry across the creek if sailing times fit. Spend the afternoon at a museum or walk the Olympic Village waterfront.

  3. Day 3: Sea-to-Sky Highway

    Leave Vancouver after breakfast. Stop at Shannon Falls and choose a second stop in Squamish for lunch or the Sea to Sky Gondola. Continue to Whistler for dinner.

  4. Day 4: Whistler mountain day

    Ski in winter or book sightseeing in summer if lifts are running. If the weather keeps you off the mountain, visit the Squamish Lil’wat Cultural Centre and explore Upper Village.

  5. Day 5: Valley Trail and the drive back

    Walk a section of the Valley Trail or go to the Audain Art Museum if open. Leave Whistler early if you’re headed to the airport. A final night in Vancouver works well before a morning flight.

Recommended stops

  • Stanley Park and Granville IslandSpread these across the two city days.
  • Shannon FallsStop near Squamish for a short walk if the park is open.
  • Whistler BlackcombUse your full Whistler day for lifts or skiing when open.

Tips for this trip

  • Pick up your rental car as you leave Vancouver if you’re using transit in the city.
  • Choose one longer Squamish outing so you still reach Whistler in time for dinner.

Frequently asked questions

How many days should you spend in each place?

Two full days in Vancouver, one mountain day, and two travel days give each place time.

Is the Sea-to-Sky Highway worth driving?

Yes if you want to stop in Squamish. Check conditions and use designated pullouts for photos.

Is a car worth renting for this route?

Yes if you want to stop in Squamish. A coach works well if you mainly want to visit both towns.

What can you do if Whistler’s lifts are closed?

Walk the Village and an open Valley Trail section or visit the cultural center.

Where can you stop for lunch on the drive?

Squamish is a natural mid-route stop before the last leg to Whistler.
