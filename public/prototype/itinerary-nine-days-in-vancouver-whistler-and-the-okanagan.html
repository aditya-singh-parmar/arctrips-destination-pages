Nine Days in Vancouver, Whistler, and the Okanagan

Vancouver to Wine Country via Whistler

Length
9 days
Towns
Vancouver, Whistler, Okanagan, Kelowna, Penticton
Best for
A group that wants both good meals and time outdoors

Vancouver, Whistler, and the Okanagan give you three different kinds of vacation in one trip. In nine days, you have time for the city, a day in the mountains, and a slower stretch by the lakes. It’s a good choice for a group that wants both good meals and time outdoors. The drive from Whistler to the Okanagan is the one to plan around. Give it a full day and decide in advance whether to fly out of Kelowna or drive back to Vancouver.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vancouver on the water

    Walk Stanley Park’s Seawall and eat dinner in the West End.

  2. Day 2: Vancouver neighborhoods

    Visit Granville Island in the morning. Spend the afternoon on the North Shore or take an easier walk around False Creek.

  3. Day 3: Sea-to-Sky Highway

    Stop at Shannon Falls and have lunch in Squamish. Stay in Whistler.

  4. Day 4: Whistler mountain day

    Take sightseeing lifts or ski in season. If lifts are closed, walk the Village and visit a museum.

  5. Day 5: Whistler to Kelowna

    Choose your route using DriveBC. If taking Highway 99 east through Pemberton and Lillooet, plan for a winding, remote stretch and fuel early. Give the drive the full day.

  6. Day 6: Kelowna and Myra Canyon

    Walk or bike the trestles, then come back into town for a meal by the lake.

  7. Day 7: Peachland and Penticton

    Stop by the water in Peachland and reach Penticton for a beach walk and dinner.

  8. Day 8: Naramata Bench

    Plan a tasting with a sober ride or follow part of the rail trail. Stay in Penticton.

  9. Day 9: Depart or drive west

    Return to Kelowna for a flight if that fits. If flying from Vancouver, use this as a drive day and plan to fly the next day.

Recommended stops

  • Granville IslandMake the market your main city stop on Day 2.
  • Shannon FallsTake a short walk on the way to Whistler.
  • Myra Canyon and Naramata BenchSpread the rail trails and tasting stops across two different days.

Tips for this trip

  • Set the Whistler-to-Kelowna route after checking road conditions.
  • If you’re flying out of Vancouver, leave Day 9 for the return drive and stay there overnight.

Frequently asked questions

Can you drive Whistler to Kelowna in winter?

Route choice depends on weather, vehicle equipment, and driver experience. Check road reports and use a route you’re comfortable driving.

Where should you spend the most time?

Give the Okanagan several nights if lakes and wine are your priority. Use an extra Whistler night if skiing is the main reason for the trip.

Where should you break up the drive to Kelowna?

Pick a fuel and lunch stop after deciding which highways to use. Services are spread out on the route via Lillooet.

Can you skip wineries in the Okanagan?

Yes. Use the Naramata day for a rail trail walk, a picnic, or a few hours at Skaha Lake.

Which nights can you cut if you only have a week?

Keep one city day in Vancouver and one main Okanagan base to trim the trip.
