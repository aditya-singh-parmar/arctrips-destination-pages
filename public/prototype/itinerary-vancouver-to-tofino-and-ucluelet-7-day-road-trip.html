Vancouver to Tofino and Ucluelet: 7-Day Road Trip

The Wild Coast Ferry Loop

Length
7 days
Towns
Vancouver, Tofino, Ucluelet, Victoria, Nanaimo

If you’re starting in Vancouver, a week gives you time to see Victoria and still spend a few nights on the Island’s west coast. Using different ferry routes in each direction saves you the drive back to where you started. It’s a good loop for travelers with a car, especially if beaches and bluff walks are the main reason for going. Book both crossings, then keep the day you drive out to the coast free of set plans.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vancouver

    Spend the day at Stanley Park and Granville Island. Sleep in Vancouver and leave the car parked for the city stops.

  2. Day 2: Ferry to Victoria

    Sail from Tsawwassen to Swartz Bay. Drive into Victoria and walk the Inner Harbour and Chinatown before dinner.

  3. Day 3: Victoria to the west coast

    Leave early and take a lunch and fuel break in Port Alberni. Check in for three nights in Tofino or Ucluelet.

  4. Day 4: Tofino

    Take a beach walk or surf lesson at the north end, then eat lunch downtown. Spend the afternoon by the harbor or back at Chesterman Beach.

  5. Day 5: Ucluelet and Pacific Rim

    Walk the Lighthouse Loop in the morning. After lunch, pick Long Beach or an open Rainforest Trail loop based on wind and rain.

  6. Day 6: Drive back to Nanaimo

    Take Highway 4 east. If parking and conditions allow, walk Cathedral Grove. Sleep in Nanaimo so the ferry day is easier.

  7. Day 7: Nanaimo to Vancouver

    Take Departure Bay to Horseshoe Bay or Duke Point to Tsawwassen, depending on where you’re headed after the crossing.

Recommended stops

  • Victoria Inner HarbourSpend your first Island afternoon here.
  • Tofino and Chesterman BeachUse one full day for town and beach time.
  • Ucluelet Lighthouse LoopPair this with one Pacific Rim stop the next day.

Tips for this trip

  • Reserve each ferry separately and save the terminal names with the bookings.
  • Sleep in Nanaimo before the return sailing so you don’t have to cross Highway 4 before check-in.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest ferry route to Tofino?

Nanaimo is the more direct Island gateway for Highway 4. This route goes through Victoria to give the south Island a day.

Can you fit a whale tour in?

Yes. Swap it for the Tofino beach block on Day 4 and keep Day 5 flexible in case the operator moves the trip.

Where should you stay on the coast?

Stay in Ucluelet for the harbor and trail or in Tofino for beach access and a walk to dinner downtown.

Can you skip Victoria and go straight to Tofino?

Yes. Cross to Nanaimo first and use the extra night near the surf.

What’s a good stop on Highway 4?

Stop for lunch in Port Alberni. Add Cathedral Grove only if access and parking are good.
