Two days from Squamish to Whistler

Length
2 days
Towns
Squamish, Whistler

Waterfall, gondola and downtown Squamish on day one, then village, gondola, lake and culture in Whistler.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Squamish

    • Shannon Falls A tall waterfall dropping down granite cliffs after a short, easy walk from the parking area, beside Stawamus Chief Provincial Park.
    • Sea to Sky Gondola Rides up for views over Howe Sound, Shannon Falls and the Stawamus Chief, with easy trails, lookout platforms and a summit lodge at the top.
    • Downtown Squamish Cafes, breweries and shops, with a relaxed local feel. Stay in Squamish or continue to Whistler.
    • Optional Chief hike or easy trail Squamish's most famous landmark. The hike is steep and challenging, with stairs, rocks, roots and three peaks; the First Peak is the shortest summit.

  2. Day 2: Whistler

    • Whistler Village The heart of the resort: walkable, with shops, restaurants, cafes, bars and lift bases, and easy to enjoy without a car.
    • Peak 2 Peak Gondola Connects Whistler and Blackcomb mountains with big alpine views. Mountain temperatures are often 5 to 10°C cooler than the village, so dress in layers.
    • Lost Lake Close to the village but peaceful and forested. Swim, picnic, walk, bike or paddle in summer; snowshoe or cross-country ski in winter.
    • Squamish Lil'wat Cultural Centre Shares the living cultures, stories, art and traditions of the Squamish Nation and Lil'wat Nation. A good rainy-day stop and a strong choice for families.
    • Dinner in the village A village meal to finish the trip.

Tips for this trip

  • Both are great in summer. Whistler is best in winter for snow; Squamish is a quieter winter stop.
