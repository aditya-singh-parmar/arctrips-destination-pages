Two days in Squamish

Length
2 days
Towns
Squamish

Views and waterfalls on day one, then one or two outdoor adventures of your choice on day two.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Classic Squamish Views

    • Shannon Falls A tall waterfall dropping down granite cliffs after a short, easy walk from the parking area, beside Stawamus Chief Provincial Park.
    • Sea to Sky Gondola Rides up for views over Howe Sound, Shannon Falls and the Stawamus Chief, with easy trails, lookout platforms and a summit lodge at the top.
    • Sky Pilot Suspension Bridge At the top of the Sea to Sky Gondola, with mountain and Howe Sound views. A gondola ticket is needed; go early for fewer people.
    • Howe Sound viewpoints Squamish sits where mountains meet the ocean. Enjoy the sound from the gondola, the Chief, highway viewpoints and waterfront areas.
    • Downtown cafes or breweries A growing food, coffee and craft beer scene. Stop for coffee, visit a brewery or browse the shops.

  2. Day 2: Outdoor Adventure (choose one or two)

    • Hike the Stawamus Chief Squamish's most famous landmark. The hike is steep and challenging, with stairs, rocks, roots and three peaks; the First Peak is the shortest summit.
    • Go mountain biking One of Canada's best mountain biking destinations, with forest trails, technical terrain and flow trails. Book a lesson or guided ride if new.
    • Alice Lake Provincial Park Lakes, forest, walking trails, picnic areas and swimming in season. Nature without a hard climb; arrive early on warm summer days.
    • Smoke Bluffs Park Best known for rock climbing, with short walks, forest trails and small viewpoints close to town.
    • Squamish Estuary Where river, marsh, mountain and ocean meet. Good for easy walks, birdwatching and quiet views.
    • Railway Museum of British Columbia British Columbia's railway history and heritage, with train equipment and exhibits in a mountainside setting.
    • Winter option: Brackendale eagle watching Bald eagles gather around the Squamish River during the salmon run. Bring binoculars and keep a respectful distance.

Tips for this trip

  • Year-round. Add Brackendale eagle watching in winter, best from late fall into winter.
