Twelve pages, one design language

Every page in the destinations prototype, grouped by how deep it sits in the tree. All twelve run on the same _system.css, which is concept D extracted verbatim, and all twelve answer to DESIGN-CONTRACT.md. The codes on each card say which ruling that page is the evidence for. Start anywhere; the tree is fully walkable in both directions from any of them.

What the codes mean

Short form of DESIGN-CONTRACT.md sections 2, 4 and 5. A card carries a code when that page is where the ruling is visible, not merely where it is obeyed: every page obeys all of them.

Season, section 2

  • S1 No month renders as a deficit
  • S2 Amber leaves the season system
  • S3 Rank, never gate
  • S4 Every month belongs to a named season
  • S5 State the trade, do not hide it
  • S6 Never a dead end on a month, two exits
  • S7 The summary leads with what is on

Navigation, section 4

  • N1 The town is the spine and never leaves
  • N2 An article wears its town's chrome
  • N3 Every reading surface ends with a next step
  • N4 Orientation without a sidebar
  • N5 The breadcrumb stays passive

Earned moments, section 5

  • Tide Today's real highs, lows and sunset
  • Rain The rainfall column at real height
  • Longitude Real city longitudes, true scale
  • Path The requested address walked against the tree
  • Real data or none. Never announced, never load bearing, at most two a screen.

How to review this

The fastest read on whether the contract landed: open plan.html and set it to November, then open things-to-do.html and set it to January. Neither should feel like an apology. If either one does, section 2 has not landed.

  • Navigation, open guide.html from tofino.html and try to get back without the browser button. The town anchor is in the sticky bar on every page below a town.
  • Season, look for amber anywhere. There is none. Emerald means in season, neutral means out of season and says so in words.
  • Earned moments, at most two a page and never labelled. Tide times are Tofino Harbour, rainfall is Environment and Climate Change Canada normals, flagged unverified and not shippable until a human checks them against the station record.
  • Dead ends, every page ends on three slots: next in this guide, more in this town, the booking path. No page ends on a footer.
