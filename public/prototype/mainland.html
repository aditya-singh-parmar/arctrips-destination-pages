The Mainland, city to mountains

Vancouver between the ocean and the North Shore mountains, the Sea to Sky Highway north to Squamish and Whistler, and Nelson above Kootenay Lake.

See the towns Plan your trip
Towns

Four towns on the Mainland

Vancouver Island

Vancouver and the Sea to Sky

The city, then one road north past Howe Sound to the granite of Squamish and the mountains of Whistler.

  1. 01 Vancouver Stanley Park, Granville Island, the Seawall, Gastown, and a mix of cuisines, arts and traditions from around the world.
  2. 02 Squamish The Stawamus Chief, the Sea to Sky Gondola, Shannon Falls, mountain biking, rock climbing and wind sports.
  3. 03 Whistler Whistler Blackcomb, skiing and snowboarding, the Peak 2 Peak Gondola, mountain biking, alpine hiking and lakes.

The Kootenays

Inland, above Kootenay Lake, with the Selkirk Mountains behind.

  1. 04 Nelson Steep old-school singletrack, Gerrard rainbow trout on Kootenay Lake, and a strong local trail culture.

Getting around the Mainland

Vancouver without a car
Waterfront Station is the central hub for the SkyTrain, SeaBus and West Coast Express. Walk, take transit, or use a False Creek ferry.
The Sea to Sky Highway
From Vancouver to Squamish, which works as a day trip, and on to Whistler, about two to two and a half hours. Winter driving needs more planning, so check road conditions before you leave.
Whistler
The village sits right at the base of Whistler and Blackcomb mountains, and is walkable once you are there.
Nelson and the Kootenays
The Balfour Ferry Terminal, gateway to the main lake, is about 30 minutes east of Nelson. Castlegar lies south on the Columbia River.
