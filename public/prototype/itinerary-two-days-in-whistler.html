Two Days in Whistler

Whistler Peaks and Lakes

Length
2 days
Towns
Whistler
Best for
Couples or families

Whistler has a different feel up on the mountain than it does by the lakes. Two days gives you time for both, with evenings free for dinner in the Village. That’s a good fit for couples or families who want a big outing and a day that’s easier on the legs. Keep the days open enough to swap them if the weather changes. If you’re coming between seasons, check what’s open before you count on the lifts.

The plan

  1. Day 1: The Village and the mountain

    Get up early for skiing in winter or gondola sightseeing when summer lifts are running. Keep lunch on the mountain if you want more time up high. If lifts are closed, spend a few hours at the Squamish Lil’wat Cultural Centre and walk through Upper Village. Book dinner near your stay.

  2. Day 2: A slower day in the valley

    In warmer weather, rent a bike and ride a section of the Valley Trail to Rainbow Park on Alta Lake. If you’d rather walk, choose Lost Lake when pedestrian routes are open. In winter, check groomed trail rules or book a snowshoe outing. End at the Audain Art Museum if it’s open and you have time.

Recommended stops

  • Whistler BlackcombPut skiing or lift sightseeing on the clearest day.
  • Rainbow ParkFollow an open Valley Trail section to Alta Lake in warmer months.
  • Audain Art MuseumKeep it in mind for a wet or smoky afternoon and check hours.

Tips for this trip

  • Stay near the Village so you can leave the car parked.
  • In winter, check where pedestrians can walk around Lost Lake before leaving the Village.

Frequently asked questions

Is two days enough in Whistler?

Yes for one mountain day and one valley day. Add a night if you also want time in Squamish.

Where should you stay without a car?

Whistler Village or Upper Village puts you close to dining and the main lift bases. Check shuttle schedules for trips farther out.

Can you swap the days around?

Yes. Take the clearest open lift day and use the other for a museum, shops, or a valley outing.

Is Whistler good for a summer weekend?

Yes. Lake parks, paved paths, and mountain sightseeing give you several ways to spend two days.

What can you do after skiing?

Have a meal in the Village, take a short walk, or visit a museum if it’s still open.
