Why go

A working harbour at the end of the road

Ucluelet sits at the tip of the peninsula south of Tofino, wrapped by the Wild Pacific Trail. It keeps a working-harbour character and tends to feel calmer than its better-known neighbour.

It makes a natural base for whale watching, wildlife tours, and long coastal walks, with easy access to Pacific Rim National Park just up the road.

Where it is
The tip of the Ucluelet Peninsula, opening onto Barkley Sound and the open Pacific.
Getting there
Forty minutes south of Tofino on the same road, or four hours from Nanaimo by Highway 4.
Known for
The Wild Pacific Trail, one of the best places on the coast to watch wildlife from land.
When to come
Gray whales pass March to May. Humpbacks are more common from midsummer to September. Whale tours run March to October.
Things to do

Four things people come to Ucluelet for

In the order most visitors do them. Each one opens a page of named places, with a good to know note and who it suits.

  1. 01

    Hiking & trails

    20 trails, from the Lighthouse Loop and the Big Beach Park Trail to the Ancient Cedars Trail and the bog boardwalks.

    Most of the Wild Pacific Trail is easy and well marked: gravel path, benches and ocean viewpoints, with stairs or uneven ground in places.

  2. 02

    Whale watching

    6 places to watch from, including the Wild Pacific Trail, Amphitrite Point Lighthouse, Barkley Sound and the Broken Group Islands.

    Gray whales on the spring migration March to May, humpbacks through September. Boat tours run about 2.5 to 3 hours. From shore, bring binoculars.

  3. 03

    Kayaking & paddling

    13 launches and routes, from sheltered Ucluelet Harbour and Little Beach out to Barkley Sound and the Broken Group Islands.

    The harbour is the beginner water and stays sheltered. Barkley Sound is the scenic sea paddle. The Broken Group is a multi-day trip with island camping.

  4. 04

    Restaurants

    15 places to eat, from Pluvio and Heartwood Kitchen to Ukee Dogs Eatery, Zoe’s Bakery & Cafe and the brewing company.

    Floathouse Patio & Grill sits on the harbourfront. Zoe’s and Barkley Cafe are the morning stops.

Questions

What people ask before they come

What is the best hike in Ucluelet?

The Wild Pacific Trail is the best-known walk in Ucluelet. The Lighthouse Loop is the best short section for a first visit, with lighthouse views, ocean views and easy walking.

Can you see whales from shore in Ucluelet?

Yes, sometimes. The Wild Pacific Trail and the Amphitrite Point Lighthouse area are among the best shore-based places to scan for whales, especially during migration season. Bring binoculars, because wildlife may be far out.

When is the best time for whale watching in Ucluelet?

March to May is best for the gray whale migration. Midsummer to September is often good for humpbacks. Orcas are possible but less predictable. Many tours run March to October and last about 2.5 to 3 hours.

Can beginners kayak in Ucluelet?

Yes, on a guided harbour tour. Ucluelet Harbour is more sheltered than the open coast, but it is still active water with boats, tides and wind, so it is best to go with a guide if you are new.

Where should I eat in Ucluelet?

Pluvio is the usual choice for fine dining and special occasions. For casual meals, Heartwood Kitchen, Floathouse Patio & Grill and Ucluelet Brewing Company are popular. Ukee Poke does a fresh seafood bowl, and Zoe’s Bakery & Cafe or Barkley Cafe are the morning stops.

Where can you camp near Ucluelet?

Ucluelet Campground is the most convenient if you want to be in town. Wya Point is quieter and beachfront, outside town. Reservations are strongly recommended, and summer sites can book months ahead.
