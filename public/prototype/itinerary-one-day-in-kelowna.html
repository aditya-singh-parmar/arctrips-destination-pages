One Day in Kelowna

Kelowna From Trestles to the Lake

Length
1 day
Towns
Kelowna
Best for
First-time visitors

Kelowna makes an easy day trip if you like a bit of country with your city. The waterfront is a good place to spend a few hours, and the old rail bridges above town are worth a look if you have a car. This day suits first-time visitors who like a walk or bike ride followed by lunch by the lake. On a hot day, get outside early. If the road to Myra Canyon is in poor shape, you can still have a good day downtown.

The plan

  1. Morning

    In dry, clear weather, drive up to the Myra Station trailhead for the Myra Canyon trestles. Walk or rent a bike in season. Even a short out-and-back gives you a look at the canyon. If the road or trail conditions are poor, start at Waterfront Park instead.

  2. Afternoon

    Have lunch downtown and walk along Okanagan Lake between Waterfront Park and City Park. If a winery is your main reason for visiting, trade part of the waterfront afternoon for a tasting in West Kelowna and arrange a sober ride.

  3. Evening

    Eat downtown and take one last walk by the lake. Stay near the water if you want to walk to dinner and skip a second drive.

Recommended stops

  • Myra StationStart here for a short out-and-back on the trestles.
  • Waterfront ParkFollow the shore toward downtown and make time to sit by the water.
  • City ParkAdd a short lakefront walk after lunch if you’re staying in town.

Tips for this trip

  • Go to the canyon early on hot days and check the access road before leaving.
  • Arrange a sober ride before you book tastings across the lake.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do Kelowna in a day without a car?

Yes if you focus on downtown and the waterfront. Arrange a ride or tour for Myra Canyon or wineries.

Is Myra Canyon worth it if you don’t bike?

Yes. Start at Myra Station and walk as far as you like before turning back. Check current road and trail conditions.

What can families do in Kelowna in one day?

Keep to the downtown lakefront for a park, a picnic, and time by the water.

Can you rent a bike at Myra Canyon?

Rentals are offered in warmer months. Check opening dates or book in advance for a busy weekend.

Is West Kelowna worth adding to a short visit?

Yes if you’re here for wine. Give it most of the afternoon so you can enjoy the tasting and the ride back.
