Ucluelet, Vancouver Island

Kayaking and paddling in Ucluelet

Thirteen places to paddle, ordered by how much water you are taking on. It starts in the harbour and ends four days out in the Broken Group Islands.

The harbour is the reason people learn to kayak here. It is sheltered enough for a first time in a boat, and it is five minutes from town. Everything after it asks for more: more skill, more planning, or a guide who reads the water for you.

Tide matters more than weather on this coast, so the readings for today sit below before the places do.

In and around the harbour

Sheltered water within sight of town. This is where a guided first paddle goes, and where you go back to when the forecast turns.

  1. 01

    Ucluelet Harbour

    Ucluelet Harbour is the best place to start if you are new to kayaking. It is sheltered, scenic, and close to town.

    On a guided harbour tour, you may paddle past docks, rocky shoreline, kelp beds, and forested edges. You may also see seals, sea lions, bald eagles, shorebirds, and sometimes black bears along the shoreline at low tide.

    Good forFirst-time kayakers · Families · Short guided tours · Wildlife watching · Calm-water paddling · Learning basic kayak skills

    Good to know. The harbour is sheltered, but it is still active water. Watch for boats, tides, and wind. A guide is helpful if you are new.

  2. 02

    Inner Harbour and Shoreline Paddles

    The inner harbour and nearby shoreline areas are good for slow paddling and getting used to the kayak. This type of trip is less about distance and more about enjoying the scenery.

    This is a good choice if you want a relaxed nature outing without committing to a full-day sea kayaking trip.

    Good forShort trips · Easy paddling · Families with older kids · Wildlife watching · Photography · Calm mornings

    Good to know. Morning can be a good time because wind is often lighter earlier in the day.

  3. 03

    Little Beach Area

    Little Beach is close to town and can be a useful area to know, but it is not always a main kayak launch for visitors. Conditions vary, and shoreline access can depend on tide, wind, and local rules.

    This area is better for experienced paddlers or guided outings when conditions are right.

    Good forShort coastal exploring · Calm-condition paddles · Local shoreline views · A quiet stop near town

    Good to know. Do not launch from rocky or rough areas unless you know what you are doing. Ask a local operator where to launch safely.

Away from the swell

Two places to go when the open coast is too much: a sheltered inlet on the park side, and the big freshwater lake up the highway.

  1. 04

    Grice Bay

    Grice Bay is near the Pacific Rim area between Tofino and Ucluelet. It is on the sheltered inlet side, not the open ocean side. This makes it a good choice for calmer paddling, bird watching, and quiet water views.

    Grice Bay is better for people who want peaceful water instead of surf, swell, and big waves.

    Good forCalm inlet paddling · Bird watching · Photography · Kayaking away from busy beaches · Quiet nature time · Families with some paddling comfort

    Good to know. Check park rules, tide, and access before going. Stay close to shore if you are new.

  2. 05

    Kennedy Lake

    Kennedy Lake is a large freshwater lake near Ucluelet and Tofino. It is not ocean kayaking, but it is worth adding because it gives visitors a calmer freshwater option.

    This can be a good place for kayaking, canoeing, and paddleboarding when the weather is calm. It is a nice choice if the ocean feels too windy or rough.

    Good forFreshwater kayaking · Canoeing · Paddleboarding · Families · Calm lake views · A break from ocean conditions

    Good to know. Lakes can still get windy and choppy. Check the forecast and stay near shore if you are new.

Barkley Sound, with a guide

Open water, islands and kelp beds, and the launches that reach them. Parts of the sound are protected and parts are exposed, which is why almost everyone goes out with someone who knows which is which.

  1. 06

    Barkley Sound

    Barkley Sound is the larger ocean area near Ucluelet. It includes islands, channels, rocky shores, coves, kelp beds, and wildlife. It is one of the main reasons Ucluelet is such a strong kayaking destination.

    Some parts of Barkley Sound are calm and protected. Other parts are more exposed and need more skill. That is why a guided tour is a smart choice for most visitors.

    Good forSea kayaking · Island views · Wildlife watching · Kelp forests · Longer day tours · Experienced beginners with a guide · Intermediate paddlers

    Good to know. Barkley Sound is bigger and more serious than Ucluelet Harbour. Wind, swell, and tides matter.

  2. 07

    Wya Point Area

    Wya Point is near Ucluelet and is known for beautiful beaches, coves, rocky shoreline, and forested scenery. It is also a popular place to stay.

    For kayaking, this area can be scenic, but it is best with local advice. Some shoreline areas can be exposed, and launch conditions may change with tide and swell.

    Good forScenic coastal paddling · Beach stays · Wildlife watching · Experienced paddlers · Guided trips · Photography

    Good to know. Ask local operators about safe launch points and current conditions.

  3. 08

    Secret Beach and Toquaht Bay

    Secret Beach, at Toquaht Bay, is one of the key kayak launch areas near Ucluelet. It is often used as a gateway to Barkley Sound and the Broken Group Islands.

    This is not in downtown Ucluelet. It is a drive away and involves rough gravel road access. But for paddlers planning a Broken Group Islands trip, it can be an important launch point.

    Good forKayak launching · Barkley Sound trips · Broken Group Islands access · Camping before or after a paddle · Multi-day kayaking · More experienced paddlers

    Good to know. Check current road, launch, parking, campground, and seasonal opening details before going. This area needs more planning than a simple harbour tour.

The Broken Group, multi-day

Boat access only, and the reason experienced paddlers come to Ucluelet at all. Five places on one trip: island channels, shell beaches and backcountry campsites.

  1. 09

    Broken Group Islands

    The Broken Group Islands are one of the best sea kayaking destinations on Vancouver Island. They are in Barkley Sound and are part of Pacific Rim National Park Reserve.

    This area has forested islands, sheltered channels, shell beaches, rocky outcrops, reefs, and backcountry campsites. It is a dream trip for many sea kayakers.

    Good forMulti-day kayaking · Kayak camping · Island hopping · Wildlife watching · Calm channels in good weather · Guided expeditions · Intermediate to advanced paddlers

    Good to know. The Broken Group Islands are boat-access only. You need proper planning, camping reservations or permits where required, navigation skills, safety gear, and current weather information.

  2. 10

    Effingham Island Area

    Effingham Island is one of the well-known island areas in the Broken Group Islands. It is a common name paddlers hear when planning a route.

    This area can offer beautiful island scenery, camping access, and classic Broken Group paddling.

    Good forIsland exploring · Camping · Wildlife watching · Multi-day routes · Experienced paddlers · Guided trips

    Good to know. Route planning matters. Distances, wind, and crossings can feel different once you are on the water.

  3. 11

    Turret Island Area

    Turret Island is another area paddlers may visit in the Broken Group Islands. The nearby channels and islands can be beautiful in calm conditions.

    It is a good example of why the Broken Group is so loved: short island distances, protected-feeling water in places, and lots of coastal detail.

    Good forIsland channels · Wildlife watching · Photography · Kayak camping routes · Intermediate paddlers · Guided trips

    Good to know. Even sheltered island areas can become windy. Plan your route with weather windows in mind.

  4. 12

    Clarke Island Area

    Clarke Island is often mentioned in Broken Group Islands trip planning because of its beach camping and beautiful setting.

    It can be a lovely place to stop on a multi-day trip, but campsite use and conditions should always be checked before you go.

    Good forKayak camping · Beach stops · Multi-day routes · Island scenery · Photography · Wildlife watching

    Good to know. Campsites can be busy in summer. Plan ahead and follow Leave No Trace rules.

  5. 13

    Loudoun Channel

    Loudoun Channel is a larger crossing area in Barkley Sound. Some paddlers use it when traveling toward the Broken Group Islands from certain launch points.

    This is not a beginner area. Wind, waves, boat traffic, and exposure can make crossings challenging.

    Good forExperienced paddlers · Route planning · Broken Group access · Guided expeditions · Strong sea kayaking skills

    Good to know. Do not treat channel crossings lightly. Check marine weather and go with proper skills or a guide.

Questions

What people ask before they book a paddle

Four answers from the Ucluelet kayaking guide.

Is Ucluelet good for kayaking?

Yes. Ucluelet is one of the best kayaking bases on Vancouver Island. It has sheltered harbour paddling, access to Barkley Sound, and routes toward the Broken Group Islands.

Can beginners kayak in Ucluelet?

Yes, beginners can kayak in Ucluelet with a guided harbour tour. The harbour is more sheltered than the open coast, but it is still best to go with a guide if you are new.

Where is the best place to kayak in Ucluelet?

Ucluelet Harbour is best for beginners. Barkley Sound is best for scenic sea kayaking. The Broken Group Islands are best for multi-day kayaking and island camping.

Should You Take a Guided Kayak Tour in Ucluelet?

For most visitors, yes. A guided kayak tour is the best way to enjoy Ucluelet safely. A guide can help with: Choosing the best route Reading tide and wind Avoiding unsafe areas Spotting wildlife Sharing local history Teaching paddle skills Managing boat traffic Handling changing weather Guided trips can be short and easy, or longer and more adventurous. You can choose a harbour tour, a Barkley Sound tour, or a multi-day Broken Group Islands trip.
