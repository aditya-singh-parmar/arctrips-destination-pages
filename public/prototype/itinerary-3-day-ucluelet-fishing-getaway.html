3-Day Ucluelet Fishing Getaway

Ucluelet Charter and Coast Escape

Length
3 days
Towns
Ucluelet, Port Alberni
Best for
A first charter or anglers who want one full day on the water

Ucluelet is a good spot for a short fishing trip because you can stay near the harbor and get to the dock without a long drive in the morning. Three days works well for a first charter or for anglers who want one full day on the water. Aim to arrive the day before and leave some room in your plans afterward for weather or fish pickup. Ask your guide what happens if the trip moves and how soon your catch can be ready to take home.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Get to Ucluelet

    Stop in Port Alberni for fuel and food. Check in near the harbor and confirm the next day’s dock, start time, and what the guide supplies. Buy your BC Tidal Waters Sport Fishing license and add a salmon stamp if you plan to keep salmon.

  2. Day 2: Guided fishing trip

    Bring layers, rain gear, lunch, and your license. Ask your guide what you can target that day based on the weather and current rules. After the trip, confirm how your catch will be cleaned, packed, stored, and picked up. Ask about fees ahead of time.

  3. Day 3: Pickup or a backup charter

    Keep your morning open for a moved charter if your guide has space, or pick up your fish and walk the Lighthouse Loop. If the charter moves to today, add a night or move your travel plans if needed for fish pickup.

Recommended stops

  • Ucluelet harborUse this as your base and confirm your guide’s exact dock.
  • Amphitrite PointWalk the Lighthouse Loop during an open hour on shore.
  • Port AlberniStop here for food and fuel before reaching the west coast.

Tips for this trip

  • Ask the guide what gear, meals, and fish services the price includes.
  • Bring an insulated cooler if you’re driving home with your catch. Check storage and travel plans with your processor before the trip.

Frequently asked questions

What can you catch in Ucluelet?

Trips may target salmon, halibut, or other species when conditions and current rules allow. Check the exact area and ask the guide before you go.

Do you need a fishing license on a charter?

Yes. Get your own tidal license before boarding. You need the salmon stamp if you retain Pacific salmon.

When is a good time to fish in Ucluelet?

Ask guides about the season for the fish you hope to catch and check current openings for your dates.

What should you wear on a fishing charter?

Pack warm layers and a waterproof outer layer, even if the forecast looks mild.

How do you get your fish home?

Confirm cleaning, packing, freezing, and pickup with your guide or processor. Ask your airline what it accepts if you’re flying.
