Three Days in Victoria and the Cowichan Valley

Harbor, Gardens, and Cowichan Backroads

Length
3 days
Towns
Victoria, Cowichan Valley, Sidney
Best for
A couple or a small group

Victoria and the Cowichan Valley make a good pair if your idea of a trip includes gardens, little shops, and a long lunch. Three days lets you enjoy the city and the peninsula before setting aside time for the towns over the Malahat. This route works well for a couple or a small group who prefer short walks and their own pace. Check opening days if you have a farm shop, tasting room, or garden you’re keen to visit.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Victoria on foot

    Walk from the Inner Harbour to Chinatown. After lunch, make your way to Beacon Hill Park and Dallas Road, or spend the afternoon at the Royal BC Museum if it’s wet. Have dinner downtown.

  2. Day 2: Gardens and Sidney

    Start at Butchart Gardens and leave time for the paths that branch off the main route. Head to Sidney for lunch and a walk by the water. If you prefer fewer stops, linger at the gardens and go back to Victoria for dinner.

  3. Day 3: Cowichan Bay and Duncan

    Drive to Cowichan Bay for coffee and a waterfront walk. In Duncan, view the downtown totem poles and read the signs as you go. For your last stop, pick a farm shop or book one winery tasting with a sober driver.

Recommended stops

  • Butchart GardensStart early enough to take your time in the Sunken Garden.
  • Cowichan BayPlan lunch near the water and a walk through the village.
  • Downtown DuncanFollow the totem poles and pause to read the signs.

Tips for this trip

  • Check farm shop and tasting hours before picking your last stop.
  • Keep the drive over the Malahat flexible if you have rain, traffic, or an early dinner booking.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit the Cowichan Valley as a day trip from Victoria?

Yes. Cowichan Bay and Duncan make a good pair. Allow extra road time on the Malahat during peak traffic.

Do you need a car?

Victoria and the gardens can work without one. A car or a booked tour helps for the valley villages and farm stops.

Is it worth staying overnight in the Cowichan Valley?

Yes if you want dinner in the valley or a slower start the next morning.

Can you visit Cowichan Bay and Duncan in one day?

Yes. Give one stop the morning and the other the afternoon.

What should you do on a rainy day in Victoria?

Visit the Royal BC Museum and keep the harbor walk short if showers are heavy.
