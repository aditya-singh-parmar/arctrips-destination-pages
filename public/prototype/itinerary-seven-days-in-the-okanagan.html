Seven Days in the Okanagan

Okanagan Lakes From Vernon to Osoyoos

Length
7 days
Towns
Okanagan, Vernon, Kelowna, Penticton, Oliver, Osoyoos
Best for
Families or active travelers who want flexible days

The Okanagan is easy to enjoy at a slow pace, especially if you like lakeside towns and trails you can walk or ride for as long as you please. A week gives you time to see how the valley changes from Vernon to Osoyoos. Staying in a few different parts of the valley keeps the drives short. This works well for families or active travelers who want flexible days. In summer, save the exposed trails for early morning and check the air quality if it’s smoky.

The plan

  1. Day 1: Vernon

    Settle in and spend the afternoon at Kalamalka Lake at a public beach or viewpoint. Eat in Vernon.

  2. Day 2: Okanagan Rail Trail

    Walk or bike an open section near Coldstream or Oyama. Check for trail gaps before setting out and choose an out-and-back if you want an easy return.

  3. Day 3: Lake Country to Kelowna

    Stop in Lake Country for lunch or one booked tasting with a sober driver. Spend the evening on Kelowna’s waterfront.

  4. Day 4: Myra Canyon

    Walk or bike the trestles if access is good. Come back downtown for lunch and an easy afternoon at City Park.

  5. Day 5: Peachland and Penticton

    Follow Highway 97 south with a coffee stop in Peachland. Spend the afternoon on the lake in Penticton and sleep at the south end of the valley.

  6. Day 6: Naramata or Skaha Lake

    Tour a winery with a sober ride or walk the Kettle Valley Rail Trail. For a beach day, swap both for Skaha Lake Park.

  7. Day 7: Oliver and Osoyoos

    Visit Osoyoos for a lake walk and the Nk’Mip Desert Cultural Centre if open. Leave time for the drive north if returning to Kelowna.

Recommended stops

  • Kalamalka LakeStart with a public beach or viewpoint near Vernon.
  • Okanagan Rail TrailWalk or bike an open out-and-back from Coldstream or Oyama.
  • Nk’Mip Desert Cultural CentreMake this your main Osoyoos stop if open.

Tips for this trip

  • Choose one main outing in each region before adding extra drives.
  • Check for trail gaps and current smoke or heat advisories before planning longer rides.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need a car in the Okanagan?

A car makes this multi-town route easier. Get a sober driver or book a tour for tasting days.

How can you adjust for heat or smoke?

Start walks early in hot weather. If smoke is an issue, check local air quality and swap outdoor plans for indoor stops if conditions warrant.

Is it better to start in Vernon or Osoyoos?

Either works. Start with the end that fits your arrival and finish close to where you need to go next.

Where should you base yourself for the southern Okanagan?

Choose Penticton for Naramata and Skaha Lake or Osoyoos for the southern lake and nearby Oliver.

Can you swim every day on this trip?

You can plan time at several lakes in warm weather. Check beach conditions and have a walk or indoor stop ready if swimming doesn’t suit the day.
