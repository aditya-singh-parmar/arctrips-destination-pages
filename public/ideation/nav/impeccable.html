Navigation direction, from the impeccable audit

One Rail

Tofino's hiking page currently says the words "Things to do" four times in the 130 pixels above the hero, across three stacked sticky bars and a breadcrumb, and the sticky stack alone eats 175px of a 900px screen. One Rail takes the position that a reading surface should carry the least persistent chrome it can get away with, and no lateral navigation at all. Two rows stay pinned: who you are reading, and which section of that town you are in. Everything else, the nine subject chips included, moves into the page body where it belongs, because a reader moves from Hiking to Kayaking after finishing Hiking, not while scrolling through it. The breadcrumb becomes the only way up, and stops competing with a back link and a tab that pointed at the same URL.

Reference pattern

Stripe's documentation chrome: one persistent product row, one short contextual row for the section you are inside, and every sibling link demoted into the article itself. Applied to a destination guide rather than an API reference, with the region band borrowed from GOV.UK's discipline of never letting a published page sit with no route into it.

Hiking and trails in Tofino

Rainforest boardwalks, beach walks and two island climbs. Most of them are short, and the muddy ones are muddy all year.

Where to walk

Eighteen walks, from the village outward

The ones you can start on foot first, then the national park trails strung along the highway, then the three that need a boat. Difficulty climbs with the distance.

Easy to moderateForest, beaches, sunset

Tonquin Trail

The Tonquin Trail is one of the best easy trails in Tofino. It starts near the village and leads through coastal forest to Tonquin Beach, Third Beach, and Middle Beach.

Good to know. The trail can be muddy after rain, and there are stairs in some areas. Wear shoes with good grip.

EasyShort beach walk

Tonquin Beach Trail

If you do not want to walk the full Tonquin Trail, you can make a shorter visit to Tonquin Beach. This is one of the closest beach walks to Tofino village.

Good to know. The beach is smaller than Chesterman or Cox Bay, so it may feel more closed in at high tide.

4 The aria-current bug, fixed

Today nav-rebuild.mjs:104 writes aria-current="page" on the Things-to-do tab of all nine subject pages, so a screen reader announces the wrong current page. Here the tab carries aria-current="true", styled with a lighter azure underline, and only the breadcrumb leaf and the active chip are page.

2 and 3 Where the chips went

Once above the hero as orientation, once at the foot as the next action. Neither is sticky, which is what returns 48px to the reading surface and removes the third repetition of the words "Things to do".
