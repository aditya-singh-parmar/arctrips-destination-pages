Title as Menu

Today the site stacks three sticky rows, 175px of chrome, and the middle row means a different thing on every route: page links in Tofino, in-page anchors on the province index, ancestors on Victoria. This direction collapses the stack into one 56px translucent bar whose shape never changes, and moves the entire navigation model into the place name itself. Tapping Tofino opens one popover carrying the town's sections, its nine subjects, and the places nearby, so every lateral move on the site is one tap from any page rather than a trip up to an index and back down. A single contextual row survives beneath it, and it is only ever one thing at a time: sections inside a town, subjects on a subject page, never both.

Reference pattern: the iOS and macOS navigation bar whose title is the menu, as in Safari tab groups and the Finder path popup Chrome: 104px against today's 175px Rejected: sidebar, breadcrumb dropdowns

Material, not slabs

Both rows are one translucent layer with content passing under it, and a scroll edge fade instead of a 1px border.

Interactive. Click a place title to open the popover, click the scrim or press Escape to close, and scroll a frame to see the chrome hold and the scroll edge fade in.

Clayoquot Sound, British Columbia

Tofino, thirteen beaches on the open Pacific

Storm-swept sand, old-growth rainforest, and the best surf in Canada. The rainfall explains all three.

Things to do in TofinoWhen to go
Why go

Rainforest on one side, open ocean on the other

Tofino sits at the western edge of Vancouver Island, where the rainforest meets long stretches of open Pacific beach. It draws surfers, storm-watchers, and travellers looking for quiet time close to the water.

Plan around the season. Summer brings calm mornings and warm evenings on the sand, while winter delivers dramatic storm watching and empty beaches.

Best months
May to September
Storm season

Hiking and trails in Tofino

Rainforest boardwalks, beach walks and two island climbs. Most of them are short, and the muddy ones are muddy all year.

Best months
Apr to Oct
Shortest
Tonquin Beach Trail
Hardest
Lone Cone
Park pass
Needed in the park
Where to walk

Eighteen walks, from the village outward

Easy to moderate · Forest, beaches, sunset

Tonquin Trail

The Tonquin Trail is one of the best easy trails in Tofino. It starts near the village and leads through coastal forest to Tonquin Beach, Third Beach, and Middle Beach.

Good to know. The trail can be muddy after rain, and there are stairs in some areas. Wear shoes with good grip.
