Navigation, round two

Three simpler ways back

Round one produced two directions and both were rejected for the same reason. Round two is subtraction only, and every direction answers one question: from a subject page, how do you get back?

Both are making things complex. Once I am in things to do, it is difficult to back.

The owner, on round one

The rules all three were held to

  • One sticky row, 64px maximum. Not two, not one plus a contextual one.
  • The way back is the most prominent control on the page, and it names its destination in words.
  • No popovers, no menus, no dropdowns in the chrome. A direction that needs a menu has failed.
  • No component appears twice. If the trail carries "up", the chrome does not.
  • Lateral movement between subjects lives in the page body, never in sticky chrome.

Round two

Same four frames in each: town, index, subject, and mobile. The back path really works, so click through it.

Direction A

One Way Back

One control, and it is the way back. The row holds a labelled pill naming its parent in words, the page title, and search. Nothing else. On a subject it says "Things to do in Tofino", on the index "Tofino", on the town "Vancouver Island". Same control, same place, every depth.

Matching the iOS navigation back button, taken literally.

Watch for: it tells you the one step up, never the whole shape of where you are.

Direction B

The Trail

Delete the place bar and promote the breadcrumb to be the navigation. The full trail is the sticky row: every ancestor a target, the leaf plain text. It is the way back, the sense of depth and the sense of place in one component, which is why there is no back button and no tabs.

Solves the jitter that got breadcrumb dropdowns rejected: four fixed slots, so nothing moves as depth changes.

Watch for: a trail is smaller and quieter than a button. Test whether it reads as the way back at a glance.

Direction C

Flat Town

The depth is the bug, so remove a level. There is no separate Things-to-do page: the town page is the index. Depth becomes exactly two everywhere, so "All of Tofino" is always correct and always means the same thing, from any subject on the site.

Hub and spoke where the hub is a real destination. One room, many exhibits, one door.

What to do with these

  1. In each one, start on the subject frame and get back to the town. Count the taps and notice how hard you had to look for the control.
  2. Then do it at 390px, which is where the live site currently has no search and no CTA at all.
  3. The three are not exclusive. Flat Town is a content decision, and either One Way Back or The Trail can sit on top of it.
