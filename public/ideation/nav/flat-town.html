Navigation, round two

Flat Town

Getting back is hard because the tree is too deep, so this direction flattens the tree instead of decorating it. There is no separate Things to do index page. The town page is the index. Every subject is one step down from the town and one step back up to it, so depth is exactly two everywhere on the site: town, subject.

Because depth is two, the single control in the 64px sticky row is always correct and always means the same thing. On a subject it reads All of Tofino. On the town page itself that same slot steps out of the town and reads All of Vancouver Island. One slot, one meaning, never a menu, never a second bar, never a breadcrumb repeating what the bar already says.

Reference pattern

A hub and spoke app shell where the hub is a real destination people return to, not a routing waypoint. The museum floor plan: one room, many exhibits, one door. Or a well made recipe site, where every recipe returns you to the collection it came from rather than to a directory listing.

  • One sticky row, 64px
  • One upward control per screen
  • No popovers, no menus, no dropdowns
  • No breadcrumb: the bar carries up
  • Lateral movement in the body only
  • Consequence: /tofino/things-to-do is absorbed into /tofino
